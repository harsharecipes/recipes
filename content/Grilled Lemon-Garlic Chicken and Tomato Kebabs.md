## Grilled Lemon-Garlic Chicken & Tomato Kebabs

**with Basil Chimichurri | By Emily and Matt Clifton**

These skewers skip the long soak in favor of a punchy, post-grill infusion. By grilling the chicken and tomatoes on separate skewers, you ensure the meat stays juicy while the tomatoes reach that perfect, blistered sweetness without falling apart.

---

### **Recipe Stats**

- **Yield:** Serves 4 to 6
    
- **Active Time:** 20 minutes
    
- **Total Time:** 20 minutes
    
- **Why it works:** Grilling tomatoes and chicken separately allows for perfect timing, while the basil chimichurri provides a fresh, acidic finish that won't make the meat "chalky."
    

---

### **Ingredients**

#### **The Kebabs**

- **2 lbs** boneless, skinless chicken thighs (or breasts), cut into 2-inch chunks
    
- **1 pint** large cherry tomatoes
    
- **3 cloves** garlic, grated or minced
    
- **3 tbsp** extra-virgin olive oil
    
- **1 tbsp** lemon zest + **1 tbsp** fresh lemon juice
    
- **1 tsp** maple syrup or honey
    
- **1 tsp** coarse kosher salt
    
- **½ tsp** freshly ground black pepper
    
- **½ tsp** crushed red pepper flakes
    

#### **Basil Chimichurri**

- **1 ¼ cups** fresh basil leaves
    
- **¼ cup** fresh flat-leaf parsley
    
- **⅓ cup** extra-virgin olive oil (plus extra for brushing tomatoes)
    
- **2 tbsp** red wine vinegar
    
- **1 small** garlic clove, grated
    
- **Kosher salt and black pepper**, to taste
    

---

### **Directions**

#### **1. Prep the Grill**

Light a charcoal grill (arrange coals on one side for two-zone cooking) or preheat a gas grill to **medium heat**. Clean and oil the grates.

#### **2. Marinate the Chicken**

In a large bowl, whisk together the garlic, olive oil, lemon zest/juice, maple syrup, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes. Prick the chicken chunks all over with a fork to help the flavors penetrate, then toss in the marinade for at least **5 minutes**.

#### **3. Pulse the Chimichurri**

While the chicken marinates, combine the basil, parsley, olive oil, vinegar, garlic, salt, and pepper in a food processor. Pulse until the herbs are coarsely chopped. Transfer to a bowl and set aside at room temperature.

#### **4. Thread the Skewers**

- **Chicken:** Thread onto metal or soaked bamboo skewers, leaving a small space between pieces for even cooking.
    
- **Tomatoes:** Thread onto separate skewers. Brush with olive oil and sprinkle with a pinch of salt.
    

#### **5. Grill**

Place both sets of skewers over direct heat.

- **Tomatoes:** Grill for about **5 minutes** until softened and blistered.
    
- **Chicken:** Grill for about **10 minutes**, turning occasionally, until lightly charred and cooked through.
    

#### **6. Serve**

Transfer skewers to a platter. Serve immediately, drizzling the fresh basil chimichurri over the hot chicken and tomatoes.
