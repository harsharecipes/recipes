[[Recipes]]

**Serves:** 8

**Nutrition per serving:** 265 calories, 7g carbs, 15g protein

---

### Ingredients

- **8 slices center-cut bacon**, chopped (half reserved for garnish)
    
- **1/2 small onion**, chopped **OR** 1 teaspoon onion powder
    
- **1 celery stalk**, chopped
    
- **2 garlic cloves**, minced
    
- **Salt & pepper**, to taste
    
- **4 cups shredded or grated cauliflower** (approx. 1/2 large head)
    
- **2 tablespoons water**
    
- **2 tablespoons flour**
    
- **2 cups chicken broth**, divided
    
- **2 cups 2% milk**
    
- **3-4 dashes hot sauce** (to taste)
    
- **2-1/2 cups (12oz) shredded sharp cheddar cheese**, divided
    
- **2 green onions**, chopped (optional, for garnish)
    

---

### Directions

1. **Prepare Slurry:** In a small bowl, whisk together the flour and 1/4 cup of the chicken broth; set aside.
    
2. **Crisp Bacon:** Sauté the chopped bacon in a large soup pot over medium heat until crisp. Use a slotted spoon to transfer the bacon to a paper towel-lined plate. Drain all but 1 tablespoon of the drippings from the pot.
    
3. **Sauté Aromatics:** Add the chopped onion (if using), celery, and garlic to the pot. Season with salt and pepper and sauté until the vegetables are tender, about 4–5 minutes.
    
4. **Steam Cauliflower:** Stir in the cauliflower and onion powder (if using). Add the water, cover with a lid, and steam until the cauliflower is tender, stirring occasionally (about 5–7 minutes).
    
5. **Boil Liquids:** Add the remaining chicken broth and the milk. Increase the heat and bring the mixture to a boil.
    
6. **Thicken:** Slowly whisk in the flour/broth mixture while stirring. Reduce heat and simmer for 3–4 minutes, or until the chowder has thickened.
    
7. **Final Seasoning:** Turn off the heat. Stir in 2 cups of the shredded cheese until smooth, then stir in half of the cooked bacon. Taste and adjust salt, pepper, or hot sauce as needed.
    
8. **Serve:** Garnish with the remaining shredded cheese, cooked bacon, and green onions if desired.
    

---

**Tip:** Using a food processor's chopping and grating blades makes prepping the vegetables and cheese much faster!
