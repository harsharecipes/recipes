## Easy Gazpacho

**Yield:** Serves 6

**Active Time:** 15 minutes

**Total Time:** 45 minutes

---

### Special Equipment

- **Blender**
    
- **Fine mesh strainer**
    

---

### Ingredients

- **6 ounces hearty bread**, crusts removed, torn into 1- to 2-inch chunks
    
- **3 pounds (about 4 large) very ripe tomatoes**, cored and cut into rough 1-inch chunks
    
- **Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper**
    
- **1/2 pound (about 1 small) cucumber**, peeled, seeded, and cut into rough 1-inch chunks
    
- **1/3 pound (about 1 small) small red onion**, peeled and cut into rough 1-inch chunks
    
- **1/3 pound (about 1 medium) green or red bell pepper**, cored, seeded, and cut into rough 1-inch chunks
    
- **1/4 cup roughly chopped fresh oregano leaves**
    
- **2 cloves garlic**, peeled and smashed
    
- **1 cup extra-virgin olive oil**, plus more for serving
    
- **2 tablespoons sherry vinegar**, plus more to taste
    
- **Chopped herbs** (parsley, chives, or oregano) for serving
    

---

### Procedures

1. **Layer and Marinate:** Place the bread in the bottom of a large bowl. Add half of the tomatoes in an even layer on top and season generously with salt and pepper. Layer the cucumbers, onion, peppers, and garlic. Sprinkle with oregano and season again with salt and pepper. Add the remaining tomatoes, seasoning once more, and drizzle with the oil and vinegar. Let the mixture rest at room temperature for 30 minutes.
    
2. **Blend:** Toss all ingredients together until evenly incorporated. Transfer half of the contents (including the liquid) to a blender and blend on high speed until completely smooth, about 3 minutes.
    
3. **Strain:** Press the blended mixture through a fine mesh strainer into a large bowl. Repeat the blending and straining process with the remaining ingredients.
    
4. **Season and Serve:** Adjust seasoning with additional salt, pepper, and vinegar to taste. Serve immediately or chill for up to three days. Garnish with chopped herbs and a drizzle of extra-virgin olive oil before serving.
