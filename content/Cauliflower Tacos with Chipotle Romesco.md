## Roasted Cauliflower Tacos with Chipotle Romesco

**By Minimalist Baker**

Smoky, roasted cauliflower meets a spicy, creamy chipotle romesco in this vibrant plant-based meal. It’s a flavor-packed, 30-minute dinner that hits all the right notes: smoky, tangy, and satisfying.

---

### **Recipe Stats**

- **Yield:** ~12 tacos
    
- **Prep Time:** 5 minutes
    
- **Cook Time:** 25 minutes
    
- **Total Time:** 30 minutes
    
- **Dietary:** Vegan, Gluten-Free, Mexican-Inspired
    

---

### **Ingredients**

#### **Roasted Cauliflower**

- **1 small head** cauliflower, chopped into bite-sized pieces
    
- **1–2 tbsp** avocado or coconut oil (or sub water)
    
- **1 ½ tsp** ground cumin
    
- **1 tsp** chili powder
    
- **1 tsp** smoked paprika
    
- **½ tsp** sea salt
    

#### **Chipotle Romesco Sauce**

- **4 cloves** garlic, skin on (for roasting)
    
- **2 cloves** raw garlic, peeled
    
- **¼ cup** raw almonds
    
- **1 can (15 oz)** fire-roasted tomatoes, drained
    
- **2 tbsp** olive oil (or sub water)
    
- **1 lime**, juiced (~3 tbsp)
    
- **1 tbsp** maple syrup (plus more to taste)
    
- **1–2 chipotle peppers** in adobo sauce
    
- **½ tsp** cumin
    
- **¼ tsp** smoked paprika
    
- **¼ tsp** sea salt (plus more to taste)
    

#### **For Serving**

- **12–15** corn tortillas
    
- **Garnishes:** Lime wedges, fresh cilantro, thinly sliced red cabbage, and pepitas (optional).
    

---

### **Instructions**

#### **1. Roast the Cauliflower**

Preheat your oven to **400°F (204°C)**. Place cauliflower on a large baking sheet and toss with oil, cumin, chili powder, paprika, and salt. Roast on the **bottom rack** for **20–25 minutes** until tender and golden brown.

#### **2. Toast Nuts & Garlic**

On a separate baking sheet, add the raw almonds and the 4 unpeeled garlic cloves. Roast on the **center rack** for **10–12 minutes** until almonds are browned and garlic is golden. Remove and peel the roasted garlic.

#### **3. Blend the Sauce**

In a high-speed blender, combine the drained tomatoes, toasted almonds, roasted garlic, raw garlic, oil, lime juice, paprika, cumin, salt, maple syrup, and chipotle peppers.

#### **4. Adjust Flavor**

Blend on high until creamy. Taste and tweak:

- **Heat:** Add more chipotle peppers.
    
- **Sweetness:** Add more maple syrup.
    
- **Zing:** Add more lime or raw garlic.
    

#### **5. Assemble and Serve**

Warm your tortillas until pliable. Spread **1–2 tbsp** of romesco sauce on each tortilla, top with a generous portion of roasted cauliflower, and finish with your choice of cilantro, cabbage, or pepitas.

---

### **Storage Tips**

- **Sauce:** Keeps in the fridge for 1 week or freezer for 1 month.
    
- **Cauliflower:** Best fresh, but keeps in the fridge for 3 days. Reheat in a **350°F (176°C)** oven to maintain texture.
