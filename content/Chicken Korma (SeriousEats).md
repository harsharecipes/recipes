## Chicken Korma

This recipe delivers a rich, aromatic Chicken Korma featuring a dual-layer flavor profile: grilled marinated chicken finished in a velvety almond and yogurt sauce.

---

### **Recipe Stats**

- **Yield:** Serves 4 to 6
    
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes (+ 2 hours marinating)
    
- **Cook Time:** 45 minutes
    

---

### **Ingredients**

#### **The Almond & Garlic Paste**

- **2 ½-inch piece** ginger, peeled and chopped
    
- **5 cloves** garlic, chopped
    
- **3 tbsp** slivered almonds
    
- **6 tbsp** water (divided)
    

#### **The Chicken & Marinade**

- **1 whole chicken**, cut into 8 serving pieces
    
- **1 ½ cups** plain yogurt (divided)
    
- **1 ½ tsp** garam masala (divided)
    
- **1 tbsp** coriander powder (divided)
    
- **1 tsp** cayenne pepper (divided)
    
- **Salt**, to taste
    

#### **The Aromatics & Sauce**

- **3 tbsp** canola oil
    
- **2 medium** onions, thinly sliced
    
- **One 2-inch** cinnamon stick
    
- **8 whole** cardamom pods
    
- **2** bay leaves
    
- **2 tbsp** golden raisins
    
- **3 tbsp** cilantro, chopped (for garnish)
    

---

### **Directions**

#### **1. Blend the Paste**

Place the ginger, garlic, and **¼ cup** of water in a blender; process until smooth. Add the slivered almonds and the remaining **2 tablespoons** of water, and blend again until a smooth paste forms. Set aside.

#### **2. Marinate and Grill**

In a medium bowl, whisk together **half** of the yogurt, garam masala, coriander, cayenne, and a pinch of salt. Coat the chicken in the mixture and marinate for **2 hours**. Skewer the chicken and grill until browned and nearly cooked through.

> **Note:** Prepare a second batch of the yogurt-spice mixture using the remaining halves of the ingredients to use later in the sauce.

#### **3. Sauté Aromatics**

In a large Dutch oven, heat the canola oil over medium heat. Add the sliced onions and cook, stirring often, until deep golden brown (about 10 minutes). Add the cardamom, cinnamon, and bay leaves to toast briefly, then remove the whole spices from the pot before proceeding.

#### **4. Build the Sauce**

Add the raisins and the blended almond-garlic paste to the onions. Cook, stirring frequently, for about one minute until a fragrant paste forms.

#### **5. Simmer**

Return the grilled chicken to the Dutch oven. Stir in the second batch of the yogurt-spice mixture. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer until the chicken is tender and fully cooked through (about 25 to 30 minutes).

#### **6. Serve**

Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve alongside basmati rice or warm naan.
