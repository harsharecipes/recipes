Here’s how she’d make it: 

1. Sauté 2 tablespoons of whole coriander seedsand two green cardamom pods in a quarter cup of ghee on medium heat until they start to brown. 
2. Add a diced onion, let that cook until translucent, and mix in a minced garlic clove, a tablespoon of chopped ginger, and a pound of fresh spinach. 
3. Once the spinach has wilted, take the pan off the heat, add a chopped Serrano chile, a quarter tablespoon of lime juice, and a teaspoon of salt, then throw everything in a blender and blitz into a chunky paste. 
4. Add that mixture back into your pan on low heat, along with a half cup of water, and fold in 6 ounces of feta cut into cubes. 
5. While that warms up, in a small pan or butter warmer, add two more tablespoons of ghee and a teaspoon of cumin seeds on medium-high heat. Once the cumin seeds start to brown, add a pinch of red chile powder, and pour the spice-oil mixture (that’s the chhonk!) over the top of the saag paneer. 
6. Serve with rice or roti.

Note: Use paneer instead of feta (didn't like the feta)
