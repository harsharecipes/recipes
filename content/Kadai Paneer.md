## Restaurant-Style Kadai Paneer

**By Swasthi**

A vibrant North Indian classic featuring succulent paneer cubes tossed with crunchy bell peppers and a fragrant, freshly ground spice blend. This "Kadai" (wok) preparation is known for its bold flavors and mildly spiced tomato base.

---

### **Recipe Stats**

- **Yield:** Serves 3 to 4
    
- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
    
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
    
- **Total Time:** 30 minutes
    
- **Cuisine:** North Indian
    

---

### **Ingredients**

_(Measurement used: 240 ml cup)_

#### **The Core**

- **200g (1 ½ cups)** Paneer, cubed
    
- **½ to ¾ cup** Capsicum (green or red bell peppers), cubed
    
- **1 to 1 ¼ cups** Tomatoes, finely chopped and deseeded
    
- **½ cup** Onions, finely chopped (optional)
    
- **1 tsp** Ginger-garlic paste (or ginger paste)
    
- **1 tbsp** Oil
    

#### **The "Kadai" Spice Blend**

- **1 tbsp** Coriander seeds (Daniya)
    
- **3 to 4** Dried red chilies
    
- **½ tsp** Garam masala
    

#### **Seasoning & Aromatics**

- **½ to 1 tsp** Red chili powder (adjust to heat preference)
    
- **¼ to ½ tsp** Kasoori methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
    
- **Pinch** of Turmeric (optional)
    
- **Salt**, to taste
    
- **Fresh coriander leaves**, chopped for garnish
    

---

### **Instructions**

#### **1. Prepare the Spice Powder**

In a blender or spice grinder, combine the **coriander seeds**, **dried red chilies**, and **garam masala**. Pulse until you achieve a slightly coarse powder. Set aside.

#### **2. Sauté Aromatics**

Heat oil in a kadai or heavy-bottomed pan. Add the ginger-garlic paste and fry until the raw aroma disappears. Add the onions and sauté until they become translucent.

#### **3. Cook the Tomato Base**

Stir in the chopped tomatoes, salt, and turmeric. Cook until the tomatoes become soft and mushy. Add your freshly ground spice powder and the red chili powder. Sauté the mixture until the oil begins to separate and the raw smell of the tomatoes vanishes.

#### **4. Add Vegetables**

Toss in the cubed capsicum. Sauté for **2 to 3 minutes**; you want the peppers to soften slightly while still retaining a bit of a crunch.

#### **5. Incorporate Paneer**

Add the paneer cubes and the kasoori methi. Stir gently to coat the paneer in the spice base. Cook for just **1 minute**—overcooking paneer at this stage can make it rubbery.

#### **6. Garnish and Serve**

Turn off the heat and stir in the freshly chopped coriander leaves. Serve hot with basmati rice, naan, or roti.

---

**Pro-Tip:** If you prefer a "gravy" version rather than a dry stir-fry, you can add a splash of warm water along with the paneer to create a thicker sauce.
