# 

## 📖 Description
This is a quick and flavorful Indian-style fried rice that comes together in just 10 minutes. Its uniqueness comes from the aromatic tempering of mustard seeds, garlic, and chilies, which infuses the rice with vibrant flavors.

## 🛒 Ingredients
- 1 1/2 tablespoons (23ml) vegetable oil
- 1 teaspoon whole black mustard seeds
- 3 medium cloves garlic (15g), roughly chopped (about 1 tablespoon)
- 4 green Thai chiles or 2 serrano peppers, stemmed and halved lengthwise
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1/2 teaspoon red chile powder
- 2 cups (12 ounces; 350g) cooked white rice
- Kosher salt
- 1 tablespoon chopped coriander leaves

## 🍳 Instructions
1. In a medium cast iron skillet, heat oil over medium-high heat until shimmering.
2. Reduce heat to medium, add mustard seeds, and cook until they pop (about 15 seconds).
3. Add garlic and chiles, cook until fragrant (about 2 minutes), adjusting heat if needed.
4. Stir in turmeric and chile powder until they color the oil (about 10 seconds).
5. Immediately add rice, stir to coat thoroughly, and season with salt.
6. Garnish with coriander leaves and serve hot.

## Notes

Rice should either be cooked fresh, spread on a tray, and allowed to cool for five minutes, or, alternatively, cooked in advance and refrigerated in a covered container for at least 12 hours and up to 3 days.
