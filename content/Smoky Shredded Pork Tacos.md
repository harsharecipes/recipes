## Smoky Shredded Pork Tacos (_Tacos de Picadillo Oaxaqueño_)

**Yields:** About 4 cups of filling, enough for 16 to 18 tacos

---

### Ingredients

**The Meat & Aromatics**

- **1 ½ pounds** boneless pork shoulder, trimmed of fat and cut into 2-inch cubes
    
- **5** garlic cloves, unpeeled
    
- **1** large white onion, diced
    
- **Salt**, about a scant ½ teaspoon
    

**Essential Quick-Cooked Tomato-Chipotle Sauce**

- **2 to 3** dried, stemmed chipotle chiles (or canned chipotles in adobo) **OR** 1 to 2 stemmed chiles pasillas oaxaqueños
    
- **A generous pound** (2 large round or 7 to 8 plum) ripe tomatoes
    
- **2 ½ tablespoons** olive or vegetable oil, or rich-tasting lard
    

**Spices, Fruits, & Nuts**

- **½ teaspoon** cinnamon, preferably freshly ground Mexican canela
    
- **¼ teaspoon** black pepper, preferably freshly ground
    
- **⅛ teaspoon** cloves, preferably freshly ground
    
- **½ cup** raisins
    
- **½ cup** slivered almonds
    

**For Serving**

- **16 to 18** corn tortillas (plus a few extra)
    
- **Hot sauce**, for serving
    

---

### Instructions

**1. Cook and Shred the Meat**

In a medium-size saucepan, cover the pork with heavily salted water. Peel and roughly chop 2 garlic cloves and add to the pot along with half of the onion. Bring to a gentle boil, skim any foam, then partially cover and simmer over medium-low until tender, about 1 ½ hours. If time permits, cool the meat in the broth. Shred the meat using your fingers or two forks.

**2. Prepare the Tomato-Chipotle Sauce**

- **Chiles:** If using dried chiles, toast on a griddle for 30 seconds, then rehydrate in hot water for 30 minutes before draining. Canned chiles just need to be removed from sauce.
    
- **Roast:** Roast the remaining 3 unpeeled garlic cloves on a griddle until soft (15 minutes); peel. Broil tomatoes until blackened on one side (6 minutes), then flip and roast the other side. Peel and collect the juices.
    
- **Finish Sauce:** Puree tomatoes, chiles, and roasted garlic in a blender. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil/lard in a saucepan, add the puree, and stir for 5 minutes until it sears and thickens. Season with salt.
    

**3. Prepare the Picadillo**

In a large skillet, heat the remaining 1 ½ tablespoons of oil/lard over medium-high. Add the shredded meat and the remaining half of the onion. Fry, stirring regularly, until the mixture is crispy and golden (12 to 14 minutes).

Sprinkle the cinnamon, pepper, cloves, and raisins over the meat, then pour in the tomato-chipotle sauce. Simmer until nearly all liquid has evaporated (4 to 5 minutes). Toast almonds in a 350°F oven for 6 to 8 minutes, then stir into the picadillo.

**4. Finish and Serve**

Steam the tortillas for 1 minute, then let them stand covered for 15 minutes. Scoop heaping tablespoons of filling into the warm tortillas. Serve immediately with hot sauce on the side.
