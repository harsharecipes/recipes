[[Recipes]]

**Yield:** Serves 10 to 14

**Active Time:** 45 minutes

**Total Time:** 6 hours

---

### Ingredients

- **2 1/2 pounds (about 2 loaves) high quality sandwich bread or soft Italian or French bread**, cut into 3/4-inch dice (about 5 quarts)
    
- **8 tablespoons (1 stick) butter**, plus more for buttering slow cooker
    
- **1 1/2 pounds sage sausage**, removed from casing
    
- **1 large onion**, finely chopped (about 2 cups)
    
- **4 large stalks celery**, finely chopped (about 2 cups)
    
- **2 cloves garlic**, minced or grated
    
- **1/4 cup minced fresh sage leaves** (or 2 teaspoons dried)
    
- **24 ounces (3 cups) low-sodium chicken or turkey broth**, preferably homemade
    
- **3 whole eggs**
    
- **1/4 cup minced parsley leaves**
    
- **Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper**
    

---

### Directions

1. **Dry the Bread:** Preheat oven to 275°F. Spread bread cubes evenly over two rimmed baking sheets. Bake until completely dried, about 50 minutes, rotating trays and stirring several times. Remove from oven and cool.
    
2. **Sauté Aromatics:** In a large Dutch oven, melt 8 tablespoons of butter over medium-high heat until foaming subsides. Add the sausage and break into fine pieces (no larger than 1/4-inch) using a whisk or potato masher. Cook until only a few bits of pink remain (about 8 minutes).
    
3. **Soften Vegetables:** Add the onion, celery, garlic, and sage. Cook, stirring frequently, until vegetables are softened (about 10 minutes). Remove from heat and stir in half of the chicken stock.
    
4. **Combine:** In a medium bowl, whisk the remaining chicken stock, eggs, and 3 tablespoons of parsley until homogeneous. Slowly pour the egg mixture into the sausage mixture while stirring constantly. Gently fold in the dried bread cubes until evenly mixed.
    
5. **Slow Cook:** Grease the inside of a slow cooker with butter. Transfer the stuffing mixture to the slow cooker and set to **low heat**.
    
6. **Finish:** Cook for 5 hours. Keep warm or serve immediately.
