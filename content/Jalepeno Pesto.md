## Trenette with Jalapeño Pesto

This spicy twist on a classic pesto uses peppers and almonds instead of the traditional basil and pine nuts.

---

### Ingredients

**Jalapeño Pesto**

- 6 fresh jalapeño peppers, cored and seeded
    
- 5 fresh serrano chiles, cored and seeded
    
- 1/2 medium red onion, chopped into 1/4-inch dice
    
- 1/2 cup sliced blanched almonds
    
- 1 cup extra virgin olive oil
    
- Salt
    

**Pasta and Assembly**

- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
    
- 6 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
    
- 1 1/2 pounds trenette (or use linguine fini pasta)
    
- 1/2 cup fresh breadcrumbs, toasted
    

---

### Instructions

1. **Make the Jalapeño Pesto:** Place the jalapeños, serranos, onions, almonds, and olive oil in a food processor and process until smooth. Season with salt to taste.
    
2. **Prep the Pasta Water:** Bring 8 quarts of water to a boil in a large pasta pot.
    
3. **Sauté the Garlic:** While the water is heating, combine the 1/4 cup olive oil and garlic in a 12- to 14-inch sauté pan. Sauté over medium-high heat until the garlic is almost brown (about 3 minutes).
    
4. **Simmer the Sauce:** Add 1 1/2 cups of the prepared jalapeño pesto to the sauté pan and bring to a simmer.
    
5. **Cook the Pasta:** When the water comes to a boil, add 2 tablespoons of salt. Drop the trenette into the boiling water and cook 1 minute less than the package instructions indicate.
    
6. **Combine:** Just before the pasta is done, carefully ladle 1/4 cup of the cooking water into the pan containing the jalapeño mixture.
    
7. **Finish and Serve:** Drain the pasta in a colander and add it to the jalapeño mixture. Toss over medium heat for about 30 seconds until nicely coated. Pour into a warmed bowl, scatter the toasted breadcrumbs over the top, and serve immediately.
    

---

### Notes

- **Serves:** 8 to 10 as a first course, or 6 as a main.
    
- **Storage:** Use any extra pesto to top cooked vegetables or as a spread for crostini. Stored in a plastic container with a snap-tight lid and covered with a thin layer of olive oil, it will keep in the fridge for 2 weeks.
