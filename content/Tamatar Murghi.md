### **Ultimate Chicken Curry (Tamatar Murghi)**

This recipe features fresh aromatics paired with a robust spice blend, rounded out with tomatoes and half-and-half.

**Ingredients**

- **Raghavan's Blend (Signature Spice Mix):**
    
    - 2 tablespoons coriander seeds
        
    - 1 tablespoon cumin seeds
        
    - 2 teaspoons black or yellow mustard seeds
        
    - 1 teaspoon black peppercorns
        
    - 1/2 teaspoon whole cloves
        
    - 12 to 15 dried red cayenne chiles (like chiles de arbol), stems discarded
        
    - 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
        
- **Curry:**
    
    - 2 tablespoons canola oil
        
    - 1 small onion, coarsely chopped
        
    - 4 medium-size cloves garlic, coarsely chopped
        
    - 4 pieces fresh ginger (about the size/thickness of a 25-cent coin; unpeeled), coarsely chopped
        
    - 2 teaspoons Raghavan’s Blend (prepared above) or store-bought Madras curry powder
        
    - 1/2 cup canned diced tomatoes with their juices
        
    - 1/2 cup half-and-half
        
    - 1 1/2 pounds skinless, boneless chicken breasts, cut into 2-inch cubes
        
    - 1 teaspoon coarse kosher or sea salt
        
    - 2 tablespoons finely chopped fresh cilantro leaves and tender stems
        

**Instructions**

1. **Prepare Spice Blend:** Place coriander, cumin, mustard seeds, peppercorns, cloves, and chiles in a spice or coffee grinder. Grind to the consistency of finely ground black pepper and stir in the turmeric.
    
2. **Sauté Aromatics:** Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Stir-fry the onion, garlic, and ginger until the onion is light caramel brown around the edges (4 to 5 minutes).
    
3. **Roast Spices and Simmer:** Sprinkle 2 teaspoons of the spice blend into the skillet and roast for 10 seconds until aromatic. Pour in tomatoes and simmer uncovered until softening and oils begin to dot the edges (5 to 7 minutes).
    
4. **Deglaze and Puree:** Pour half-and-half into the skillet to deglaze, then transfer the mixture to a blender. Puree until smooth and saffron orange-hued.
    
5. **Cook Chicken:** Return the sauce to the skillet and add the chicken and salt. Simmer covered over medium-low heat until the chicken is cooked through and juices run clear (12 to 15 minutes).
    
6. **Finish:** Sprinkle with fresh cilantro and serve.
