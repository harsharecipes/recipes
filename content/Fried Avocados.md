## Fried Avocado Tacos With Chipotle Cream, Cabbage, and Pickled Red Onions

These vegetarian tacos feature beer-battered avocado slices that provide a crisp exterior contrasting with a creamy center.

**Yield:** Serves 4

**Active Time:** 30 minutes

**Total Time:** 30 minutes

---

### Special Equipment

- **Wok, deep fryer, or Dutch oven**
    

---

### Ingredients

- **1 1/2 quarts peanut, vegetable, or canola oil**
    
- **3/4 cup all-purpose flour**
    
- **1/4 cup cornstarch**
    
- **Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper**
    
- **1/4 teaspoon baking powder**
    
- **3/4 to 1 cup light beer**
    
- **2 avocados**, peeled, pitted, and cut into 8 to 12 wedges each
    
- **16 warm corn tortillas**
    
- **1 cup store-bought or homemade salsa verde**
    
- **1/2 head finely shredded green cabbage**
    
- **1/2 cup chipotle cream**
    
- **1/2 cup pickled red onions**
    
- **1 thinly sliced Serrano chile**
    
- **1/2 cup roughly chopped fresh cilantro leaves**
    
- **2 limes**, cut into 8 wedges
    
- **Radishes**, for serving
    

---

### Procedures

1. **Heat the Oil:** Heat the oil in your chosen vessel to 375°F. Adjust the heat as needed to maintain this temperature.
    
2. **Prepare the Batter:** In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, cornstarch, 1/2 teaspoon salt, 1/4 teaspoon pepper, and baking powder until homogenous. Rapidly whisk in 3/4 cup of beer until a loose, lumpy batter forms (similar to thick paint). Add more beer if necessary, but do not overmix.
    
3. **Coat the Avocados:** Season the avocado wedges generously with salt and pepper. Drop them into the batter and turn to coat.
    
4. **Fry:** Carefully lower each coated wedge into the hot oil one at a time. Fry, turning occasionally, until light golden brown and crisp (about 3 minutes). Maintain an oil temperature between 350°F and 375°F.
    
5. **Drain:** Use tongs or a wire mesh spider to transfer the avocados to a paper towel-lined bowl. Season with salt immediately and toss to drain.
    
6. **Assemble and Serve:** Stack tortillas in pairs (8 stacks total). Divide the salsa verde and shredded cabbage among the tortillas and top with the fried avocado. Drizzle with chipotle cream and garnish with pickled red onions, Serrano chile, and cilantro. Serve immediately with lime wedges and radishes.
