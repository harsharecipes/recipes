Gobhi Aloo Kee Sabzi
Serves 4

Ingredients
The Base & Spices

3 tablespoons canola oil
1 teaspoon nigella seeds (kalaunji; optional)
1 teaspoon cumin seeds
Rounded 1/4 teaspoon coriander seeds
2-inch piece fresh ginger, peeled and chopped
1/8 teaspoon ground cumin
1/8 teaspoon cayenne pepper
1 fresh hot green chile, chopped
1 teaspoon salt, or to taste
2 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro

The Vegetables

1 1/2 pounds red boiling potatoes, peeled, halved, and sliced lengthwise about 1/2 inch thick
1 small to medium head cauliflower, cored and cut into medium florets

Instructions
Bloom the Seeds: Combine the oil, nigella (if using), cumin seeds, and coriander seeds in a large wok, kadai, or frying pan over medium-high heat. Cook, stirring, until the cumin turns golden brown (about 2 to 3 minutes).

Sauté Ginger & Potatoes: Add the ginger and cook, stirring, for 1 minute. Add the potatoes and stir well to coat with the spices.

Initial Potato Cook: Cover and cook until the potatoes have browned some and have begun to soften and stick to the bottom of the pan (8 to 10 minutes). Stir 3 or 4 times during this process.

Note: If they brown too quickly, turn the heat down. If not browning, raise the heat.

Add Cauliflower: Add the cauliflower florets, ground cumin, cayenne, and fresh chile. Stir to coat the cauliflower with the spices.

Brown the Vegetables: Cover and cook, stirring and scraping the bottom of the pan 3 or 4 times, until the potatoes are well browned (about 10 minutes).

Final Steam: Sprinkle with salt, cover, and continue cooking until the cauliflower is nicely browned and the vegetables are tender (5 to 10 more minutes). Stir every 3 to 4 minutes.

Tip: If they stick badly or burn, add a teaspoon of water and scrape with a spatula to loosen the crispy bits.

Finish: Sprinkle with chopped cilantro and serve hot.
