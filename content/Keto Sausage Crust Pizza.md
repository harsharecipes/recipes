## Keto Sausage Crust Pizza

**Inspired by Lou Malnati’s "Crustless Pizza"**

For those who love deep-dish flavor but want to skip the carbs, this recipe uses seasoned Italian sausage as the base. It’s savory, filling, and significantly more budget-friendly than the Chicago restaurant original.

---

### **Ingredients**

#### **The Sausage Crust**

- **1 lb** Bulk Italian sausage (hot or mild)
    
- **1 tsp** garlic powder
    
- **1 tbsp** Italian herb seasoning
    
- **2 tbsp** grated Parmesan cheese
    
- **½ tsp** black pepper
    

#### **Low-Carb Tomato Sauce**

- **1 can (28 oz)** low-carb tomato sauce
    
- **1 tbsp** minced garlic
    
- **1 tbsp** olive oil
    
- **2 tsp** Italian herb seasoning
    
- **½ tsp** kosher salt
    
- **½ tsp** black pepper
    

#### **Toppings**

- **2 cups** Provolone/Mozzarella cheese blend, shredded
    
- **Sliced pepperoni**
    
- **Optional:** Extra Parmesan and Italian seasoning for garnish
    

---

### **Directions**

#### **1. Prepare the Crust**

Preheat your oven to **350°F**. Take a round pizza pan and press the bulk sausage out evenly until it reaches the edges.

> **Note:** Do not grease the pan. The sausage has enough fat to prevent sticking, and adding oil could cause a flare-up.

#### **2. Season and Par-Bake**

Sprinkle the garlic powder, Italian seasoning, Parmesan, and black pepper over the raw sausage. Bake until the sausage is almost cooked through (it should look grey but not yet browned). Remove from the oven and carefully drain off any excess grease.

#### **3. Simmer the Sauce**

While the crust bakes, heat olive oil in a sauce pot over medium heat. Add the garlic and seasonings, cooking until golden brown. Pour in the tomato sauce and simmer on low for **15 minutes**.

#### **4. The First Layer**

Sprinkle **½ cup** of the shredded cheese blend directly onto the par-cooked sausage crust. Return it to the oven briefly just until the cheese is melted. This creates a "barrier" for the sauce.

#### **5. Assemble and Finish**

Remove the pan from the oven. Spread your desired amount of tomato sauce over the melted cheese. Layer on the pepperoni and top with the remaining shredded cheese.

#### **6. Bake and Serve**

Place the pizza back in the oven and bake until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown. Let the pizza cool for a few minutes to allow the sausage crust to set before slicing. Top with extra Parmesan and herbs to taste.
