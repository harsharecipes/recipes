## MAKHANI SAUCE

**Makes $5\frac{1}{2}$ cups**

_Makhani_ is Hindi for "with butter." A lot of recipes for this classic butter-and-cream-based tomato sauce call for sugar and honey. Ours doesn't do that. We cook the tomatoes slowly, simmering them to bring out their sweetness and reduce the acidity of the fruit. Use the ripest tomatoes you can find; this will give the sauce a brighter, redder appearance. If you find the finished sauce too acidic because of your tomatoes, add a tablespoon of honey at the end.

This sauce always comes in handy, so make a double batch and freeze it, but omit the salt and cream. To use, thaw the sauce and reheat it over medium heat. When it comes to a boil, stir in the salt and cream. Then the sauce is ready to add to Paneer Makhani (page 129) or Chicken Makhani (page 186).

---

### Ingredients

- **$1\frac{1}{2}$ pounds** medium tomatoes, quartered
    
- **8** medium garlic cloves
    
- **4** medium fresh Thai green chilies
    
- **1-inch piece** fresh ginger, coarsely chopped
    
- **$\frac{1}{2}$ cup** dried fenugreek leaves
    
- **1 quart** water
    
- **1 cup** tomato paste
    
- **1 stick (4 ounces)** unsalted butter, cut into 1-inch cubes
    
- **1 tablespoon** salt
    
- **$\frac{3}{4}$ cup** heavy cream
    

---

### Instructions

1. **Initial Simmer**: In a large saucepan, combine the tomatoes, garlic, green chilies, ginger, fenugreek leaves, and water. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Reduce the heat to medium and simmer for **30 minutes**, stirring occasionally. Remove from the heat and let cool for **10 minutes** to reduce steam buildup.
    
2. **Puree**: Transfer the contents of the pan to a blender and puree until smooth. Start on low speed and gradually increase it to allow steam to escape safely.
    
3. **Strain**: Clean out the saucepan and strain the sauce through a coarse sieve back into it. Use a wooden spoon to press down on the solids to extract as much liquid as possible, then discard the solids.
    
4. **Secondary Simmer**: Return the pan to medium heat. Add the tomato paste and butter and bring to a rolling simmer, stirring occasionally. Simmer for **30 minutes**, stirring from time to time. (If you’re freezing the sauce for later, stop here and freeze for up to 1 month. The salt and cream will be added when you reheat the sauce.)
    
5. **Finish & Store**: Stir in the salt and cream. Return the sauce to a boil and remove from the heat. If not using right away, let cool completely. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to **2 days**.
