## THE GRETA (SUGAR COOKIE SQUARES)

**Makes about 2 dozen 2-inch squares**

---

### Ingredients

- **1/2 pound (2 sticks) unsalted butter**, at room temperature, plus more for the pan
    
- **2 cups sugar**
    
- **2 large eggs**
    
- **1/2 cup grapeseed or other neutral oil**
    
- **1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract**
    
- **3 cups all-purpose flour**
    
- **1 teaspoon kosher salt**
    
- **1 teaspoon baking soda**
    
- **1/2 cup whole milk**
    

#### The Cinnamon Sugar

- **1 tablespoon sugar**
    
- **1 teaspoon ground cinnamon**
    

---

### Directions

1. **Prep:** Heat the oven to 350°F. Grease a $9 \times 13$-inch baking pan.
    
2. **Cream Butter and Sugar:** Combine the butter and sugar in the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment. Cream on high until homogenous, about 3 minutes. Add the eggs, oil, and vanilla and mix until just combined, about 1 minute.
    
3. **Mix Dry and Wet:** Add the flour, salt, and baking soda, mixing until just combined, about 30 seconds. Mix in the milk until just combined, about 30 seconds.
    
4. **Assemble:** Spread the dough in an even layer in the prepared pan. Mix together the sugar and cinnamon in a small bowl and sprinkle evenly over the cookie dough.
    
5. **Bake:** Bake for 20 to 25 minutes for a slightly underbaked cookie, or for another 3 to 5 minutes if you prefer a firm cookie.
    
6. **Cool:** Cool completely in the pan before cutting into squares.
