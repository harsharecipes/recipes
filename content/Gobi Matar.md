## Gobi Matar (Cauliflower and Green Peas)

**By Kanan**

This healthy and homey dry vegetable dish (sabzi) is made from cauliflower and green peas in a savory tomato base. It is the perfect accompaniment to roti or paratha.

---

### **Recipe Stats**

- **Yield:** 2 servings
    
- **Prep Time:** 20 minutes
    
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
    
- **Total Time:** 40 minutes
    
- **Cuisine:** Indian
    

---

### **Ingredients**

- **2 cups** cauliflower, separated into small florets
    
- **1 cup** green peas (fresh or frozen)
    
- **2 small** tomatoes (or 1 cup pureed)
    
- **2 tbsp** oil
    
- **½ tsp** cumin seeds
    
- **1 tsp** ginger paste (or freshly grated)
    
- **1 tsp** garlic paste (or freshly grated)
    
- **1** green chili, chopped finely
    
- **½ tsp** turmeric powder
    
- **1 tsp** red chili powder
    
- **1 ½ tsp** coriander powder
    
- **Salt**, to taste
    
- **½ cup** water
    
- **½ tsp** garam masala
    
- **½ tsp** amchur powder (dried mango powder)
    
- **2 tbsp** cilantro (coriander leaves), finely chopped
    

---

### **Instructions**

#### **1. Sauté Aromatics and Spices**

- Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat. Once hot, add cumin seeds and let them sizzle.
    
- Add ginger paste, garlic paste, and chopped green chilies. Sauté for about a minute or until the raw smell of the ginger and garlic dissipates.
    
- Stir in the turmeric, red chili powder, and coriander powder, mixing immediately to prevent the spices from burning.
    

#### **2. Add Vegetables**

- Right away, add the cauliflower florets and green peas. Mix quickly to ensure the spices at the bottom of the pan do not burn.
    
- Stir in the tomato puree and salt.
    

#### **3. Simmer and Cook**

- Add the water and mix well.
    
- Cover the pan and cook until the cauliflower is tender all the way through.
    
- Stir once or twice during cooking to ensure it doesn't stick. If the mixture becomes too dry, add a splash of water and continue cooking.
    

#### **4. Finish and Season**

- Once the vegetables are soft, add the garam masala and amchur powder.
    
- Mix well and cook for an additional 1–2 minutes until the water is absorbed and the thick gravy coats the cauliflower and peas (it should be semi-dry).
    

#### **5. Garnish and Serve**

- Turn off the stove, sprinkle with chopped cilantro, and mix.
    
- Serve immediately or keep covered until ready to eat.
