[[Recipes]]

**Yield:** Serves 4

**Active Time:** 15 minutes

**Total Time:** 15 minutes

---

### Ingredients

- **1/2 pound (about 8 strips) bacon**, coarsely chopped
    
- **5 tablespoons vegetable oil**, divided
    
- **1/2 medium onion**, finely chopped (about 1/2 cup)
    
- **1/2 teaspoon minced or grated ginger**
    
- **1 medium clove garlic**, minced (about 1 teaspoon)
    
- **2 cups cooked white rice**
    
- **1/2 cup thawed frozen green peas**
    
- **3 eggs, beaten**, plus **4 un-cracked whole eggs**
    
- **2 tablespoons soy sauce**
    
- **1/2 teaspoon chili paste**
    
- **1/2 tablespoon toasted sesame oil**
    
- **1 tablespoon vegetable oil**
    
- **Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper**
    
- **Sliced scallions and/or chopped cilantro leaves**
    

---

### Procedures

1. **Crisp the Bacon:** Cook bacon in a large skillet over medium heat until crisp, about 10 minutes total. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate to drain.
    
2. **Sauté Aromatics:** Pour excess bacon fat into a small bowl, rinse and wipe out the skillet, then return to high heat. Add one tablespoon of bacon fat and heat until shimmering. Add onion and cook, stirring frequently, until translucent (about 4 minutes). Add ginger and garlic and cook until fragrant (about 1 minute).
    
3. **Fry the Rice:** Add 3 more tablespoons of bacon fat to the skillet (use vegetable oil if there is not enough fat). Add cooled rice and cook, stirring and shaking constantly to separate grains and break up lumps, for about 2 minutes. Add peas and mix until evenly combined.
    
4. **Scramble Eggs:** Move the rice to the sides of the pan to create a well in the center. Add the beaten eggs; once they start to set, stir them into the rice.
    
5. **Season:** Add soy sauce, chili paste, and sesame oil. Stir and toss until well coated, then mix in the reserved bacon. Cover and remove from heat.
    
6. **Fry Whole Eggs:** Heat 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil in a large non-stick skillet over high heat until shimmering. Add the 4 whole eggs and fry to your desired doneness, seasoning with salt and pepper.
    
7. **Serve:** Divide the rice between 4 plates and top each with a fried egg. Sprinkle with scallions and/or cilantro and serve immediately with extra chili sauce.
