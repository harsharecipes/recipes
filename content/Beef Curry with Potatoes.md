[Youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M_BFNn6bpEc)

INGREDIENTS: 
1 kg beef (any cut is fine , I prefer to use cheap cuts for this dish)
 4  medium potatoes
2 medium onions 
3 tbsp oil
Whole spices: 3 cardamoms, 5 cloves, 1 inch cinnamon stick 
1/2  tsp turmeric powder
1 tsp cumin powder
1 tbsp Kasmari chilli powder
1 tbsp coriander powder
1/4 tsp Garam masala (optional)
2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
1/2 can crushed tomatoes or 2 fresh tomatoes
coriander to garnish

Prep
Marinate the meat with, ginger garlic paste, cumin powder corinder powder, turmeric, chili powder, salt and 2 tsp oil. Mix thoroughly  and keep it aside.
Sliced the onions thinly
Peeled the potatoes, cut into halves and soak them in cold water to prevent browning.

Cooking
Heat up the oil at medium heat, add the whole spices and let it bloom.
Add the onions and fry till soft and translucent
Add the marinated meat to the pan and mix well.
Fry till oil separates then add the tomato puree and salt(1/2 tsp) and fry for another five minutes.
Add 2.5 cups of warm water and potatoes .
Cover and let it cook till beef is tender and stare occasionally.
Once its done finish it with garam masala . cook for a minute 
Turn of the heat and garnish with coriander.
Enjoy with rice , naan or bread.
