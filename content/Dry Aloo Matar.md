## Dry Aloo Matar

**By Dassana**

A tangy, mildly spiced North Indian sabzi featuring baby potatoes and green peas. This dry preparation is highly versatile and works beautifully as a main or side dish.

---

### **Recipe Stats**

- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
    
- **Cook Time:** 25 minutes
    
- **Total Time:** 35 minutes
    
- **Yield:** Serves as a side or main
    
- **Cuisine:** North Indian
    

---

### **Ingredients**

_(Note: 1 cup = 250 ml)_

- **10-12** baby potatoes (skin intact, halved) or **3** regular medium potatoes
    
- **1.5 cups** fresh or frozen peas (matar)
    
- **2 to 3 tbsp** oil
    
- **1 tsp** cumin seeds (jeera)
    
- **1 inch** ginger, chopped
    
- **1 or 2** green chilies, chopped
    
- **½ to 1 tsp** red chili powder
    
- **1 tsp** coriander powder
    
- **1 tsp** cumin powder
    
- **1 tsp** dry mango powder (amchur)
    
- **1 tsp** chaat masala powder
    
- **½ tsp** garam masala powder
    
- **Salt** (rock salt, black salt, or regular), to taste
    
- **Optional:** Coriander leaves for garnish and lime wedges for serving
    

---

### **Instructions**

#### **1. Sauté Aromatics and Base**

- Wash the potatoes thoroughly and rinse the peas.
    
- Heat oil in a pan and fry the cumin seeds until they sizzle.
    
- Add the potatoes and peas; stir and sauté for 7–8 minutes.
    
- Add the chopped green chili and ginger, stirring for another minute.
    

#### **2. Add Spices and Simmer**

- Add the red chili powder, cumin powder, and coriander powder. Stir for one minute.
    
- Pour in **½ to ¾ cup** of water.
    
- Cover the pan tightly and simmer for 20–25 minutes, or until the vegetables are cooked through.
    
- **Note:** Check periodically to ensure the water hasn't completely evaporated; if it dries out before the veggies are done, add another **¼ to ½ cup** of water.
    

#### **3. Season and Finish**

- Once the potatoes and peas are tender and the mixture is totally dry, add the salt, dry mango powder, and garam masala.
    
- Mix well, then add the chaat masala.
    
- Garnish with fresh coriander leaves.
    

#### **4. Serve**

- Serve hot with rotis or parathas. It also makes an excellent side dish for dal-rice or kadhi-rice combinations. Serve with lime wedges if desired.
    

---

### **Tips & Variations**

- **Pressure Cooker Method:** You can pressure cook this dish for 2–3 whistles after adding the water.
    
- **Vrat/Fasting Version:** Use rock salt (sendha namak) and omit any spices not typically used during your family's religious fasting days.
    
- **Adjust Heat:** The proportion of spice powders can be easily adjusted to suit your personal heat tolerance.
