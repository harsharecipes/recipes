[[Recipes]]

**Yield:** 4

**Active Time:** 15 minutes

**Total Time:** 1 hour, 45 minutes

---

### Special Equipment

- **Grill**
    
- **Hand-mixer**
    

---

### Ingredients

- **4 medium Idaho baking potatoes**, scrubbed
    
- **4 ounces (1/2 block) cream cheese**, room temperature
    
- **1/2 cup sour cream**
    
- **8 ounces (1 block) extra-sharp cheddar cheese**, shredded and divided
    
- **4 slices peppered bacon**, cooked and finely crumbled
    
- **3 tablespoons finely minced fresh chives**
    
- **Kosher salt**, to taste
    
- **4 tablespoons butter**, melted
    

---

### Directions

1. **Bake the Potatoes:** Adjust the oven rack to the middle position and preheat to 375°F. Poke each potato several times with a fork and place them directly on the rack. Bake until the skin starts to crisp and the flesh is soft, about 1 hour. Remove from the oven.
    
2. **Prep the Grill:** While the potatoes are baking, preheat a gas grill to 375°F.
    
3. **Make the Filling:** When the potatoes are cool enough to handle, slice off the upper third. Scoop the interior into a medium bowl, leaving a 1/4-inch layer of flesh in the skin. Add the cream cheese and sour cream; beat with a hand-held mixer until fluffy, about 30 seconds.
    
4. **Fold and Season:** Using a wooden spoon, fold in the bacon, chives, and all but 1/4 cup of the cheddar cheese. Season with salt to taste.
    
5. **Assemble:** Brush the outer skins of the potatoes with melted butter. Fill the hollowed skins generously with the potato mixture and sprinkle the reserved 1/4 cup of cheese on top.
    
6. **Grill:** Place the potatoes in the center of the grill over direct heat. Cook until the skins are crackly, the interiors are hot, and the cheese is bubbly, about 7 to 10 minutes.
    
7. **Serve:** Remove from the grill and let cool for 3 minutes before serving.
