[[Recipes]]

This flavorful, slow-cooked chuck roast is braised in a rich blend of coffee, spices, and chipotle for maximum tenderness. The recipe also includes a savory blended sauce for turning the beef into traditional rolled enchiladas.

---

### **Ingredients**

#### **The Beef & Braise**

- **2 lbs** chuck roast, cut into two equal pieces (ensure some fat remains)
    
- **1 tbsp** bacon grease, lard, or vegetable oil
    
- **1** yellow onion, chopped
    
- **4 cloves** garlic, chopped
    
- **½ cup** brewed coffee
    
- **2 tbsp** Worcestershire sauce
    
- **2 tbsp** apple cider vinegar
    
- **2** canned chipotle chiles in adobo sauce
    
- **2 tsp** ground cumin
    
- **¼ tsp** ground cinnamon
    
- **1 cup** water
    
- **Salt and black pepper**, to taste
    

#### **The Finishing Sauce**

- **Reserved broth** from the pot
    
- **Rehydrated pasilla and ancho chiles** (plus water for blending)
    
- **Oil**, for the pot
    
- **Masa harina or flour**, for thickening
    
- **Cumin, oregano, and allspice**, to taste
    

#### **For Enchiladas**

- **Corn tortillas**
    
- **Lard**, for softening tortillas
    
- **Grated cheese**
    
- **Diced onion**
    

---

### **Directions**

#### **1. Sear and Sauté**

- Preheat the oven to **275°F**.
    
- Season the roast with salt and pepper.
    
- In a large ovenproof pot, heat bacon grease over medium-low heat. Brown the meat for **5 minutes per side**, then remove and set aside.
    
- Add the chopped onion to the pot and cook until it begins to brown (about 5 minutes). Stir in the garlic and cook for **30 seconds** more.
    

#### **2. The Slow Braise**

- Return the roast to the pot. Add the coffee, Worcestershire sauce, vinegar, chipotle chiles, cumin, cinnamon, and water.
    
- Bring to a boil on the stovetop, then cover and transfer to the oven.
    
- Cook for **3 to 3 ½ hours** until the meat is fork-tender and falling apart.
    

#### **3. Shred the Beef**

- Remove the meat from the pot, leaving the broth behind to cool.
    
- Using two forks, shred the beef into long strands. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
    

#### **4. Prepare the Sauce**

- Blend the cooled broth, onions, garlic, and chipotle chiles from the pot with the rehydrated pasilla/ancho chiles and water until smooth.
    
- In a medium pot, heat oil over medium-low heat and whisk in masa harina or flour for **30 seconds**.
    
- Pour in the blended sauce and add cumin, oregano, and allspice.
    
- Cook over low heat for **5 to 10 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until smooth.
    
- Toss the shredded beef with **2 tablespoons** of this sauce.
    

#### **5. Assemble and Bake Enchiladas**

- Preheat the oven to **350°F** and grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
    
- Soften tortillas by heating them one at a time in a skillet with hot lard until pliant.
    
- Fill each tortilla with about **¼ cup** of beef, roll tightly, and place seam-side down in the dish.
    
- Pour the remaining sauce over the top and garnish with cheese and diced onion.
    
- Bake for **15 minutes** until the cheese is bubbling and lightly browned.
