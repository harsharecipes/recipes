[[Recipes]]

**Recipe courtesy of Alton Brown**

- **Yield:** 4 servings
    
- **Level:** Easy
    
- **Total Time:** 1 hr 10 min
    
- **Prep:** 30 min
    
- **Cook:** 30 min
    
- **Inactive:** 10 min
    

---

### Ingredients

- **1 whole egg**
    
- **1 tablespoon water**
    
- **1-ounce dried apple rings**
    
- **1 whole pork tenderloin**, approximately 1 pound
    
- **4 1/2 ounces thinly sliced prosciutto ham**
    
- **1/4 teaspoon kosher salt**
    
- **1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper**
    
- **1 teaspoon chopped fresh thyme leaves**
    
- **1 teaspoon all-purpose flour**
    
- **1 sheet puff pastry**, thawed completely
    
- **1 tablespoon whole-grain mustard**
    

---

### Directions

1. **Preheat:** Place a rack in the upper third of the oven and heat to 400°F.
    
2. **Prep Egg Wash and Apples:** Whisk the egg and water in a small bowl and set aside. Process the apple rings in a mini food processor for 30 to 45 seconds until they reach a medium dice.
    
3. **Prepare the Pork:** Trim the tenderloin of excess fat and silver skin. Slice the tenderloin lengthwise into two separate pieces and lay them head to tail so the ends are of equal size when rejoined.
    
4. **Wrap in Prosciutto:** On a $12 \times 16$-inch piece of parchment paper, overlap the prosciutto to create a solid layer as long as the tenderloin. Place a second piece of parchment on top and use a rolling pin to adhere the pieces together. Remove the top parchment, season the ham with salt, pepper, and thyme, and place the pork in the center. Spread the dried apples between the two pork pieces and press them together. Use the parchment to wrap the prosciutto tightly around the pork.
    
5. **Wrap in Pastry:** On a floured surface, roll the puff pastry to $12 \times 14$ inches. Spread mustard in the center and place the prosciutto-wrapped pork on top. Fold the pastry over the pork to enclose it, brushing edges with egg wash to seal. Flip the package over so the double-thickness seam is on the bottom and pinch the ends to seal.
    
6. **Bake:** Brush the entire pastry with egg wash. Place on a parchment-lined sheet pan and bake for 25 to 30 minutes, or until the pork reaches an internal temperature of at least 140°F.
    
7. **Rest and Serve:** Transfer to a cooling rack and let rest for 10 minutes before slicing.
