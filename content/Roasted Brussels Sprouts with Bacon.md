[[Recipes]]

This popular, simple side dish combines the nutty bitterness of sprouts with the smoky flavor of bacon, finished with a sweet balsamic glaze.

**Yield:** Serves 4–6

**Total Time:** 45–50 minutes

---

### Ingredients

- **1½ pounds Brussels sprouts**
    
- **2–3 tablespoons melted ghee** (or fat of choice)
    
- **4 bacon slices**, diced
    
- **Kosher salt and pepper**, to taste
    
- **Aged balsamic vinegar**, for drizzling
    

---

### Directions

1. **Prep the Oven and Sprouts:** Preheat your oven to **400°F**. Trim the ends and remove any old outer leaves from the sprouts.
    
2. **Cut and Season:** Cut the Brussels sprouts in half. Toss them in a bowl with your melted fat of choice, salt, and pepper.
    
3. **Arrange:** Spread the sprouts in a single layer on a foil-lined baking sheet. Sprinkle the diced bacon evenly over the top.
    
4. **Roast:** Place the tray in the oven and roast for **30 to 35 minutes**. Set a timer for 10-minute intervals to rotate the pan and flip the sprouts and bacon for even browning.
    
5. **Finish:** Once roasted, check for seasoning. Drizzle with aged balsamic vinegar and serve immediately.
    

---

**Tip:** This roasting technique also works perfectly with cauliflower or broccoli!
