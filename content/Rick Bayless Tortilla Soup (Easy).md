[[Recipes]]

**Yield:** 6

---

### Ingredients

- **1 to 2 canned chipotle chiles in adobo**, plus sauce to taste
    
- **1 can (15-ounce) tomatoes**
    
- **2 tablespoons vegetable or olive oil**
    
- **1 medium white onion**, sliced 1/4 inch thick
    
- **3 cloves garlic**, peeled
    
- **2 quarts chicken broth**
    
- **1 large epazote sprig** (optional)
    
- **Salt**, to taste
    
- **4 boneless, skinless chicken breast halves** (about 1 1/4 pounds total), cut into 1/2-inch cubes
    
- **1 large ripe avocado**, peeled, seeded, and cut into 1/4-inch cubes
    
- **1/2 cup shredded cheese** (Mexican such as Chihuahua, quesadilla, or asadero, or Monterey Jack)
    
- **6 ounces roughly broken tortilla chips**
    
- **1 large lime**, cut into 6 wedges
    

---

### Procedures

1. **Sauté and Blend:** Heat the oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion and garlic and cook, stirring frequently, until golden (about 7 minutes). Transfer these aromatics to a blender along with the chipotles and tomatoes, and blend until smooth.
    
2. **Concentrate and Simmer:** Return the blended mixture to the pan and cook over medium-high heat, stirring often to avoid burning, until it reduces to the consistency of tomato paste. Stir in the broth and epazote (if using), then reduce to a simmer. Cook for 15 minutes to allow flavors to meld, then season with salt to taste.
    
3. **Cook Chicken and Assemble:** Add the chicken cubes to the pot and simmer for 3 to 5 minutes until just cooked through but still tender. While the chicken simmers, divide the avocado, cheese, and tortilla chips among soup bowls.
    
4. **Serve:** Ladle the hot soup into the prepared bowls and serve immediately with a squeeze of lime.
