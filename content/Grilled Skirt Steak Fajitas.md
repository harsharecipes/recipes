[[Recipes]]

**Yield:** Serves 4 to 6

**Active Time:** 45 minutes

**Total Time:** 4 to 12 hours

---

### Special Equipment

- **Grill**
    
- **Cast iron pan**
    

---

### Ingredients

- **1/2 cup soy sauce**
    
- **1/2 cup lime juice** (from 6 to 8 limes)
    
- **1/2 cup canola oil**
    
- **1/4 cup packed brown sugar**
    
- **2 teaspoons ground cumin seed**
    
- **2 teaspoons freshly ground black pepper**
    
- **1 tablespoon chili powder**
    
- **3 medium cloves garlic**, finely minced (about 1 tablespoon)
    
- **2 pounds trimmed skirt steak**, cut crosswise into 5- to 6-inch pieces
    
- **1 large red bell pepper**, stemmed, seeded, and cut into 1/2-inch-wide strips
    
- **1 large yellow bell pepper**, stemmed, seeded, and cut into 1/2-inch-wide strips
    
- **1 large green bell pepper**, stemmed, seeded, and cut into 1/2-inch-wide strips
    
- **1 white or yellow onion**, cut into 1/2-inch slices
    
- **12 to 16 fresh flour or corn tortillas**, hot
    
- **Guacamole, Pico de Gallo, sour cream, shredded cheese, and salsa**, for serving (if desired)
    

---

### Procedures

1. **Marinate the Steak:** Whisk together soy sauce, lime juice, oil, brown sugar, cumin, pepper, chili powder, and garlic in a medium bowl. Reserve 1/2 cup of this marinade in a separate bowl. Place the steaks in a gallon zipper-lock bag, add the remaining marinade, and seal while removing air. Massage the bag to coat and refrigerate for 3 to 10 hours, turning occasionally.
    
2. **Marinate Vegetables:** Toss the peppers and onions in the bowl with the reserved 1/2 cup of marinade and refrigerate until needed.
    
3. **Prep the Grill:** Remove steaks from the marinade and wipe off any excess. Prepare one chimney full of charcoal and arrange the lit coals on one side of the grate to create two-zone heating. Preheat the covered grill for 5 minutes, then clean and oil the grate.
    
4. **Grill the Steak:** Place a large cast iron skillet on the cooler side of the grill. Grill the steaks on the hot side, flipping and covering every minute until well-charred. Aim for an internal temperature of **115°F–120°F** for medium-rare or **125°F–130°F** for medium. Let the steaks rest, tented with foil, for 10 to 15 minutes.
    
5. **Cook Vegetables:** Move the cast iron skillet to the hot side of the grill for 2 minutes. Add the pepper and onion mix and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and charred (about 10 minutes). Pour any juices from the resting meat into the skillet and toss with the vegetables.
    
6. **Serve:** Transfer vegetables to a warm platter. Thinly slice the meat against the grain and arrange with the vegetables. Serve immediately with hot tortillas and your choice of condiments.
