### **Vietnamese Shaking Beef (Bo Luc Lac)**

This dish features beef cubes that are "shaken" in a hot wok until charred on the edges and cooked through.

**Ingredients**

- **Beef**: 1 lb of beef (filet mignon or a cheaper cut), cut into 1-inch cubes.
    
- **Vegetables/Greens**: 1 or 2 sliced tomatoes and 1 bunch of watercress, mixed field greens, or lettuce.
    
- **Aromatics**: 2 cloves of garlic, minced.
    
- **Marinade**:
    
    - 2 tsp Nuoc Mam (Vietnamese Fish Sauce) or soy sauce.
        
    - 1/2 tsp salt.
        
    - 1/2 tsp ground black pepper.
        
    - 1/2 tsp sugar.
        
    - _Optional_: 1 or 2 minced chilies.
        
- **Topping**: Vietnamese Vinegared Onions (Hanh Dam).
    

**Instructions**

1. **Marinate**: Dice the beef into 1-inch cubes. Combine the fish sauce, salt, pepper, and sugar to create a marinade. Toss the beef in the marinade and let it sit at room temperature.
    
2. **Prepare Serving Plate**: Wash the greens and slice the tomatoes. Arrange them on a plate and top with the vinegared onions (drain the onions first, as you do not want the vinegar juices on the plate).
    
3. **Cook**: Heat a wok over high heat and drizzle in a bit of oil. Add the minced garlic.
    
4. **"Shake" the Beef**: Add the beef to the wok, leaving the extra marinade behind to ensure the beef chars properly rather than becoming soggy. "Shake" the beef in the wok until the edges are charred and it is cooked to your preference.

### **Accompanying Recipe: Vietnamese Vinegared Onions (Hanh Dam)**

These are used as a topping for the Shaking Beef.

**Ingredients**

- 1 onion, sliced as thin as possible.
    
- About 1/2 cup rice or white vinegar.
    
- 1 tsp white sugar.
    

**Instructions**

1. Place the thinly sliced onions in a shallow bowl.
    
2. Dissolve the sugar into the vinegar.
    
3. Pour just enough of the vinegar mixture over the onions to cover them.
    
4. Let them sit for about 15 minutes, mixing occasionally to ensure they absorb the vinegar and soften.
