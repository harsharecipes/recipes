## Crispy Potato, Chorizo, and Green Chili Hash With Avocado and Eggs

**Yield:** Serves 4

**Active Time:** 40 minutes

**Total Time:** 40 minutes

---

### Special Equipment

- **Large cast iron or non-stick skillet**
    

---

### Ingredients

- **1 1/2 pounds russet or Yukon gold potatoes**, cut into 3/4- to 1/2-inch dice
    
- **2 tablespoons distilled white vinegar**
    
- **Kosher salt**
    
- **4 ounces Spanish chorizo**, diced
    
- **1/4 cup vegetable, canola, or olive oil**, divided (plus more as needed)
    
- **1/2 teaspoon ground cumin**
    
- **1/4 teaspoon ground paprika**
    
- **Freshly ground black pepper**
    
- **6 scallions**, white and light green parts cut into 1-inch lengths; greens sliced and reserved separately
    
- **1 poblano pepper**, cut into 1/2-inch dice
    
- **4 eggs**
    
- **1/4 cup roughly chopped fresh cilantro leaves**
    
- **1/2 small avocado**, diced
    
- **1/2 cup homemade or store-bought salsa verde** (optional, for serving)
    
- **12 warm corn tortillas** (optional, for serving)
    

---

### Procedures

1. **Parboil Potatoes:** Preheat the oven to 375°F. Place the potatoes in a large saucepan and cover with 2 quarts of cold water. Add the vinegar and 2 tablespoons of kosher salt. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce to a simmer and cook until barely tender (about 5 minutes after boiling). Drain in a colander for 2 minutes.
    
2. **Crisp the Chorizo:** Place the chorizo in a cast iron skillet over medium heat. Cook, stirring, until the chorizo is crisp and the fat has rendered (about 10 minutes). Use a slotted spoon to move the chorizo to a large bowl, leaving the fat in the skillet.
    
3. **Brown the Potatoes:** Add 3 tablespoons of oil to the fat in the skillet and heat over medium-high until shimmering. Add the drained potatoes and cook, stirring occasionally, until deep brown and crisp (about 20 minutes). Stir in the cumin and paprika for 30 seconds. Season with salt and pepper, then transfer the potatoes to the bowl with the chorizo.
    
4. **Char Aromatics:** Add the remaining tablespoon of oil to the skillet over high heat. Add the scallion whites/light greens and poblano pepper; cook until charred (about 2 minutes). Season with salt and pepper, then add to the bowl with potatoes and chorizo. Toss everything together and return the mixture to the skillet. Make 4 wells in the hash.
    
5. **Prep and Add Eggs:** Break one egg into a small bowl and slide it into a fine mesh strainer over the sink to drain excess loose whites. Carefully place the egg into one of the wells in the skillet. Repeat with the remaining three eggs.
    
6. **Bake and Garnish:** Drizzle the eggs with a few drops of oil, season with salt and pepper, and transfer the skillet to the oven. Bake until whites are barely set but yolks are still runny (about 10 minutes). Remove from the oven and garnish with sliced scallion greens, cilantro, and avocado. Serve immediately with salsa and tortillas if desired.
