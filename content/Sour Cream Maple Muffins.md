# Sour Cream - Maple Muffins

Ingredients:


1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour (plain)

2 teaspoons baking powder

1 teaspoon baking soda

1/2 teaspoon salt

1/2 cup unsalted butter at room temp

3/4 cup maple syrup

1 cup sour cream

1 egg

1/2 cup chopped pecans


1. Preheat oven to 400F, butter muffin tins

2. In small bowl, stir and toss together the flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt. Set aside. In a medium bowl, beat the butter until smooth, then slowly add the maple syrup, beating constantly. Beat in the sour cream and egg. Stir in the pecans. Add the combined dry ingredients and stir until just blended.

3. Spoon into the prepared muffin tins, filling each cup about 2/3rds full. Bake until a toothpick inserted in the center of a muffin comes out clean, 15-18 minutes. Cool in the tins for 5 minutes, then remove


Makes about 16 standard muffins.
