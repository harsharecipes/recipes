[[Recipes]]

**Serves:** 4

---

### Ingredients

- **1½ cups traditional couscous**, rinsed and drained
    
- **½ teaspoon sea salt**
    
- **1¼ cups warm water**
    
- **2 tablespoons safflower or olive oil**
    
- **2 tablespoons butter**, in small pieces
    

---

### Instructions

1. **Preheat and Hydrate:** Preheat the oven to 350°F. Tip the couscous into an ovenproof dish. Stir the salt into the water and pour it over the couscous. Leave the couscous to absorb the water for about 10 minutes.
    
2. **Prep for Baking:** Using your fingers, rub the oil into the grains to break up the lumps and air them. Dot the butter over the surface and cover with a piece of foil or wet greaseproof paper.
    
3. **Heat Through:** Put the dish in the oven for about 15 minutes to heat through.
    
4. **Finish and Serve:** Fluff up the grains with a fork and serve the couscous from the dish, or tip it onto a plate piled high in a pyramid.
    

> **Note on Proportions:** 1 lb of couscous made with wheat or corn flours serves about 6 people. Use the following proportions of salted water to couscous: 2 cups of water for 2 cups of barley couscous; 2½ cups of water for 2 cups of seffa (ultrafine) couscous.
