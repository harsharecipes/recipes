# **Cincinnati Style Chili**

1 qt water

2 lbs ground beef

3 tsp cinnamon

3 tsp cumin

1 yellow onion finely diced

2 tsp Worcestershire sauce

2 cloves garlic

6 tbsp chili powder (ancho is fine)

3 tsp pepper

3 tsp salt

3 tsp allspice

1.5 tsp pepper flakes

6 oz can tomato paste

2 tbsp vinegar

2 lg bay leaves

  

Meat in pot with water, break up meat finely, add ingredients, boil, simmer for 2-3 hours (longer better)
