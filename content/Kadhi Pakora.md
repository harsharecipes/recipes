## Kadhi Pakora: A North Indian Comfort Classic

This dish is the definition of warmth and nostalgia—a perfect balance of tangy yogurt, aromatic spices, and golden, crispy pakoras. Whether you like it thick and creamy or light and runny, this recipe is easily adjustable to your soul-food standards.

---

## **Ingredients**

#### **The Base (Gram Flour & Yogurt Mix)**

- **250g** Sour yogurt (well-whisked)
    
- **75g** Besan (gram flour)
    
- **700ml** Water (plus more to adjust thickness)
    
- **2 tsp** Turmeric powder
    
- **1 tsp** Kashmiri red chilli powder
    
- **Salt** (to taste; start light)
    

#### **The Pakoras (Fritters)**

- **1 ½ cups** Gram flour
    
- **1 medium** Onion, sliced
    
- **2–3** Green chillies, finely chopped
    
- **1 handful** Fresh spinach
    
- **2 tsp** Ginger, finely chopped
    
- **¼ tsp** Ajwain (carom seeds)
    
- **1 sprig** Curry leaves, finely chopped
    
- **Oil** (for deep frying)
    
- **Water** (as needed for a sticky batter)
    

#### **The Sauté & Simmer**

- **Oil** (for cooking)
    
- **½ tsp each:** Mustard seeds, fenugreek seeds, and coriander seeds
    
- **1** Dried red chilli
    
- **1 medium** Onion, sliced
    
- **1 clove** Garlic (~5g), minced
    
- **1 small piece** Ginger (~6g), minced
    
- **2 sprigs** Fresh curry leaves
    
- **1 tbsp** Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
    

#### **The Final Tempering (Tadka)**

- **Ghee** (for richness)
    
- **1 ½ tsp** Cumin seeds
    
- **3** Small round chillies (or 2 dried red chillies)
    
- **1 pinch** Hing (asafoetida)
    
- **1 tsp** Kashmiri red chilli powder
    

---

## **Instructions**

1. **Prep the Base:** Whisk the yogurt, besan, turmeric, and chilli powder until completely smooth. Gradually stir in the water to ensure there are no lumps. Set aside.
    
2. **Fry the Pakoras:** Combine pakora ingredients in a bowl. Add water a little at a time until you have a thick, sticky batter. Deep-fry spoonfuls of the mix until golden brown and crispy. Set aside.
    
3. **Sauté the Aromatics:** In a heavy-bottomed pot, heat oil. Add mustard, fenugreek, and coriander seeds. Once they pop, add the garlic, ginger, onion, dried chilli, and fresh curry leaves. Sauté until the onions are soft and translucent.
    
4. **Simmer the Kadhi:** Pour in the prepared yogurt-besan mixture. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low. Let it simmer for **15–20 minutes**, stirring occasionally.
    
    > **Note:** If it gets too thick, add a splash of water. It should be silky and coat the back of a spoon.
    
5. **Finish the Curry:** Season with salt and crush the kasuri methi between your palms before stirring it into the pot.
    
6. **The Tempering:** In a small pan, heat ghee. Add cumin seeds, hing, and dried chillies. Turn off the heat and stir in the Kashmiri red chilli powder (this prevents it from burning). Immediately pour this sizzling ghee over the simmering kadhi.
    
7. **Assemble:** Transfer the kadhi to a serving bowl. Gently place the pakoras on top right before serving so they stay slightly crisp, and garnish with fresh coriander.
