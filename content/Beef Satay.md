## Beef Satay with Spicy Almond Sauce

**Serves 6 to 8**

This paleo-friendly take on Southeast Asian satay replaces flank steak for traditional meats and uses a nutty almond butter base for the dipping sauce instead of peanut sauce.

---

### **Ingredients**

#### **For the Skewers**

- **1 (1 ½-pound)** flank steak, trimmed
    
- **¼ cup** coconut aminos
    
- **2** garlic cloves, minced
    
- **1 tsp** kosher salt
    
- **½ tsp** pepper
    
- **30** wooden skewers
    

#### **For the Dipping Sauce**

- **½ cup** creamy almond butter
    
- **¼ cup** hot tap water
    
- **2 tbsp** lime juice
    
- **1 ½ tbsp** hot sauce
    
- **1 tbsp** coconut aminos
    
- **2** scallions, sliced thin
    
- **1 tbsp** minced fresh cilantro
    
- **1** garlic clove, minced
    

---

### **Instructions**

#### **1. Prepare the Meat**

Slice the steak in half with the grain, then slice each piece against the grain on a bias into ¼-inch-thick slices. Lay pieces in a single layer between two sheets of plastic wrap and pound until roughly ⅛ inch thick.

> **Tip:** Freeze the flank steak for 15 minutes before slicing to make it easier to handle.

#### **2. Marinate**

In a large bowl, combine coconut aminos, garlic, salt, and pepper. Stir in the beef, cover, and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes or up to 1 hour.

#### **3. Make the Dipping Sauce**

Whisk all dipping sauce ingredients together in a serving bowl until smooth; set aside.

#### **4. Skewer and Prep for Broiling**

Set wire racks in 2 aluminum foil-lined rimmed baking sheets. Adjust an oven rack to 3 inches from the broiler element and heat the broiler. Pat the beef dry with paper towels and weave 1 piece of meat onto each wooden skewer.

#### **5. Broil**

Lay skewers evenly on the prepared racks with the skewer ends facing the center of the sheet. Cover the exposed skewer ends with a narrow strip of aluminum foil to prevent burning, securing it by crimping the edges. Broil until the meat is browned, 8 to 10 minutes, flipping the skewers halfway through.

#### **6. Serve**

Serve the warm skewers immediately with the prepared almond dipping sauce.
