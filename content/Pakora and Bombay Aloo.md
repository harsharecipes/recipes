# 🍛 Combined Script: Kadhi Pakora & Bombay Aloo

This script is designed for a home kitchen with at least three burners. Total time: **50–60 minutes**.

## **Master Prep List (Mise en Place)**
*Prepare these groups before you light a single flame.*

### **Group A: The Produce**
- **Potatoes:** 500g, diced into 1.5-inch pieces.
- **Onions:** 3 total (1 sliced for Pakoras, 1 sliced for Kadhi, 1 finely chopped for Bombay Aloo).
- **Aromatics:** 6 cloves Garlic (minced), 3-inch piece Ginger (minced), 4 Green Chillies (chopped), 3 sprigs Curry Leaves.
- **Tomato:** ¾ cup finely chopped and deseeded.

### **Group B: Kadhi Base**
- **Whisk together:** 250g sour yogurt + 75g besan + 2 tsp turmeric + 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder. 
- **Stir in:** 700ml water until perfectly smooth. Set aside.

### **Group C: Pakora Batter (Dry)**
- **In a large bowl:** 1.5 cups besan + 1 onion (sliced) + 2 green chillies + handful fresh spinach + 2 tsp ginger + ¼ tsp ajwain + chopped curry leaves + salt. 
- *Do not add water yet.*

---

## **The Kitchen Script**

### **Phase 1: Foundation (0:00 – 0:15)**
1. **Potatoes (Burner 1):** Place potatoes in a pot with salted water. Bring to a rolling boil.
2. **Kadhi Prep:** Give your yogurt-besan mixture one final whisk. 
3. **Check Veg:** Ensure all your aromatics are separated into their respective piles.

### **Phase 2: Sauté & Simmer (0:15 – 0:25)**
4. **Drain Potatoes:** Once fork-tender, drain potatoes and let them steam dry.
5. **Start Kadhi (Burner 2):** Heat oil in a heavy pot. Add mustard, fenugreek, and coriander seeds. Once they pop, add 1 sliced onion, 1/3 of your ginger/garlic, and 1 sprig curry leaves. Sauté for 3 mins until soft.
6. **Simmer Kadhi:** Pour in the yogurt-besan base. Bring to a boil, then lower heat to a simmer. **Set a 20-minute timer.**

### **Phase 3: Frying & Crisping (0:25 – 0:35)**
7. **Pakoras (Burner 1 / Deep Pan):** Heat oil for deep frying. Add a splash of water to your Pakora bowl to make a thick, sticky batter. Fry spoonfuls until golden. Set aside on paper towels.
8. **Crisp Potatoes (Burner 3 / Large Pan):** Heat 2 tbsp oil. Fry the boiled potatoes until the skins are golden and crisp. Remove potatoes to a plate.

### **Phase 4: Masala & Texture (0:35 – 0:45)**
9. **Aloo Masala (Burner 3):** In the potato pan, add mustard/cumin seeds. Add chopped onions and sauté until golden. Add remaining ginger, garlic, and green chili.
10. **Spice Mix:** Stir in the ground spices (turmeric, chilli powder, cumin, coriander, fennel powder, garam masala). Add tomatoes and sauté until soft and aromatic.
11. **Check Kadhi:** Stir the Kadhi. Add salt and crushed kasuri methi. If too thick, add a splash of hot water.

### **Phase 5: The Grand Finale (0:45 – 0:55)**
12. **Combine Aloo:** Return crispy potatoes to the masala pan with ½ cup hot water. Cover and cook on low for 5 minutes.
13. **Kadhi Tadka (Burner 1 / Small Pan):** Heat 2 tbsp ghee. Add cumin seeds, hing, and dried chillies. Turn off heat, add 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder, and **immediately pour the sizzling ghee into the Kadhi.**
14. **Assemble:** Gently place the pakoras into the Kadhi right before serving to maintain texture.

---

## **Plating**
- Garnish both dishes with fresh coriander.
- Serve with steaming Basmati rice or soft rotis.
