## Bombay Potatoes (Bombay Aloo)

Bombay potatoes are Indian-style spiced potatoes cooked with onions, tomatoes, curry leaves, and various spices. This recipe yields approximately 3 servings.

- **Prep Time:** 10 minutes
    
- **Cook Time:** 35 minutes
    
- **Total Time:** 45 minutes
    

---

### Ingredients

**Base Ingredients**

- **Potatoes:** 500 grams (1.1 pounds), baby or regular
    
- **Onions:** 1 cup fine chopped (1 large)
    
- **Tomatoes:** 3/4 cup fine chopped and deseeded
    
- **Aromatics:** 1 sprig curry leaves, 1 teaspoon grated/minced ginger, 1 teaspoon grated/minced garlic, and 1 green chili (optional)
    
- **Oil:** 2 to 3 tablespoons
    

**Spices**

- **Seeds:** 1/4 teaspoon mustard seeds and 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds (optional)
    
- **Ground Spices:**
    
    - 1/2 teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder or paprika
        
    - 1/8 to 1/4 teaspoon turmeric
        
    - 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
        
    - 1/2 teaspoon cumin powder
        
    - 1/2 to 3 1/2 teaspoons coriander powder
        
    - 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon fennel powder (optional)
        
- **Salt:** 1/4 teaspoon (adjust to taste, plus more for boiling)
    

---

### Instructions

#### 1. Boil the Potatoes

- **Prep:** Wash potatoes thoroughly. Peel if desired, otherwise brush the skins well. Dice large potatoes into 1 1/2-inch pieces or poke whole baby potatoes with a fork.
    
- **Cook:** Add potatoes to a pot with 3 cups of water and 1 teaspoon salt. You may optionally add turmeric, garam masala, and red chili powder to the water for extra flavor.
    
- **Drain:** Bring to a rolling boil, then reduce to medium heat. Boil until fork-tender but not mushy. Remove from water and cool completely.
    

#### 2. Fry the Potatoes

- Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a hot pan.
    
- Add the boiled potatoes and fry until they are golden and crisp.
    
- Remove the fried potatoes to a plate.
    

#### 3. Prepare the Masala

- Add another tablespoon of oil to the same pan. Add mustard and cumin seeds; once they splutter, stir in the curry leaves.
    
- Add the onions, salt, and green chilies. Saute on medium-high heat until the onions turn golden.
    
- Lower the heat and add the ginger and garlic, sauteing for one minute.
    
- Stir in all ground spices: red chili powder, turmeric, garam masala, cumin powder, coriander powder, and fennel powder.
    
- Add the tomatoes and saute until they are soft and mushy. Continue to saute for a few more minutes until the masala is aromatic.
    

#### 4. Combine and Simmer

- Return the fried potatoes to the pan and pour in 1/2 cup of hot water.
    
- Mix well, bringing the potatoes to the center and covering them with the onion-tomato masala.
    
- Cover and cook on low heat for 5 to 6 minutes to allow the potatoes to absorb the flavors. Avoid overcooking to prevent mushiness.
    

#### 5. Serve

- Turn off the heat and garnish with coriander leaves.
    
- Serve with rice, roti, or chapati. Alternatively, mash them slightly to use as a filling for wraps or sandwiches.
