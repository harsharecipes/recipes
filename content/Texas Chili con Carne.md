## Real Texas Chili Con Carne

**By J. Kenji López-Alt | The Food Lab**

This is the "real deal" Texas Red: chunky, fall-apart tender beef chuck and a deep, complex sauce made from a custom blend of dried chilies. No beans, no fillers—just science-backed flavor.

---

### **Recipe Stats**

- **Yield:** Serves 6 to 8
    
- **Active Time:** 45 minutes
    
- **Total Time:** 3 hours
    
- **Heat Level:** Moderately hot (adjustable)
    

---

### **Ingredients**

#### **The Chili Base**

- **3 whole** sweet dried chilies (Costeño, New Mexico, or Choricero), stems/seeds removed
    
- **2 small** hot dried chilies (Arbol or Cascabel), stems/seeds removed
    
- **3 whole** rich fruity dried chilies (Ancho, Mulatto, Negro, or Pasilla), stems/seeds removed
    
- **2 whole** Chipotle chilies in adobo, plus **2 tbsp** sauce
    
- **2 quarts** low-sodium chicken broth (divided)
    

#### **The Meat & Aromatics**

- **4 lbs** beef chuck, trimmed and cut into thick steaks
    
- **2 tbsp** vegetable oil
    
- **1 large** onion, finely diced
    
- **4 cloves** garlic, grated
    
- **Kosher salt and black pepper**, to taste
    

#### **Spices & Seasoning**

- **1 tbsp** ground cumin
    
- **2 tsp** dried oregano
    
- **½ tsp** powdered cinnamon
    
- **¼ tsp** ground allspice
    
- **2 tbsp** Asian fish sauce (for umami)
    
- **2 to 3 tbsp** masa harina (for thickening)
    
- **2 tbsp** apple cider vinegar
    
- **Hot sauce**, to taste
    

#### **For Serving**

- Cilantro, chopped onions, scallions, grated cheese, avocado, and warm tortillas.
    

---

### **Directions**

#### **1. Prepare the Chili Purée**

Toast the dried chilies in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat until fragrant (2–5 minutes), or microwave them on a plate for 30 seconds. Place toasted chilies in a microwave-safe bowl with **half** of the chicken broth and the chipotles. Microwave until simmering (about 5 minutes), let sit for 5 minutes, then blend until completely smooth.

#### **2. Sear the Beef**

Season the beef steaks with salt and pepper. Heat oil in the Dutch oven over high heat until smoking. Sear **half** of the steaks until deeply browned (about 6 minutes per side). Transfer to a cutting board. Once cool enough to handle, cube both the seared and the remaining raw steaks into 1.5- to 2-inch chunks.

#### **3. Sauté Aromatics**

In the same pot, sauté the onions until softened (2 minutes). Add garlic, cinnamon, cumin, allspice, and oregano; cook for 1 minute until fragrant.

#### **4. The Long Simmer**

Return all meat to the pot. Stir in the chili purée and the remaining quart of chicken broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer. Cover with the lid slightly ajar.

- **Stovetop:** Simmer for 2.5 to 3 hours.
    
- **Oven:** Alternatively, cook in a 200°F to 250°F oven with the lid ajar.
    

#### **5. The Finishing Touches**

Stir in the fish sauce, masa harina, and vinegar. Add hot sauce and salt to taste.

> **Pro-Tip:** For the best flavor, let the chili cool and refrigerate overnight. Reheat the next day to allow the flavors to fully marry.

#### **6. Serve**

Garnish generously with cilantro, onions, cheese, and avocado. Serve with warm tortillas.
