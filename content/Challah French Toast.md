### Variation: Almond-Crusted with Orange

This recipe uses only egg yolks to avoid the "sulfur" taste of egg whites, resulting in a rich, custard-like interior and a crispy exterior. The almond-crusted variation adds a nutty crunch and a hint of citrus.

---

### Ingredients

**The Bread**

- 8 large slices good-quality **challah** (or hearty white sandwich bread), 1/2-inch thick
    

**The Custard Base**

- 1 1/2 cups **whole milk**, warmed (about 80°F)
    
- 3 large **egg yolks**
    
- 3 tablespoons **light brown sugar**
    
- 2 tablespoons **unsalted butter**, melted (plus 2 tbsp for the skillet)
    
- 1 tablespoon **vanilla extract**
    
- 1/2 teaspoon **ground cinnamon**
    
- 1/4 teaspoon **table salt**
    

**Almond-Orange Variation (Optional)**

- 1/2 cup **slivered almonds**
    
- 1 tablespoon **light brown sugar**
    
- 1 tablespoon **Triple Sec**
    
- 1 teaspoon **grated orange zest**
    

**For Serving**

- Maple syrup
    

---

### Instructions

#### 1. Prep and Dry the Bread

- Adjust oven rack to the middle position and heat oven to **300°F**.
    
- Place bread slices on a wire rack set in a rimmed baking sheet. Bake until almost dry throughout (centers should remain slightly moist), about **16 minutes**, flipping halfway through.
    
- Remove from rack and cool for 5 minutes.
    
- Reduce oven temperature to **200°F** and return the baking sheet/rack to the oven to keep finished toast warm later.
    

#### 2. Mix the Custard

- In a large bowl, whisk together the warmed milk, egg yolks, brown sugar, cinnamon, 2 tablespoons melted butter, salt, and vanilla.
    
- **For Almond Variation:** Add the Triple Sec and orange zest to this mixture.
    
- Transfer the mixture to a 13x9-inch baking pan.
    

#### 3. Prepare the Almond Crust (Optional)

- Process the slivered almonds and 1 tablespoon brown sugar in a food processor until coarsely ground (about 12–15 pulses). You should have about 1/2 cup of crumbs.
    

#### 4. Soak the Slices

- Soak bread in the milk mixture until saturated but not falling apart, about **20 seconds per side**.
    
- **For Almond Variation:** After soaking, sprinkle 1 tablespoon of the almond mixture over one side of each slice.
    
- Lift slices with a slotted spatula, let excess liquid drip off, and set on a platter.
    

#### 5. Cook

- Heat 1/2 tablespoon of butter in a 12-inch skillet over **medium-low heat**.
    
- Once foaming subsides, add 2 slices of bread. (If using the **almond crust**, place the nut-mixture side down first).
    
- Cook until golden brown, **3 to 4 minutes per side**.
    
- Transfer cooked slices to the wire rack in the 200°F oven. Wipe the skillet clean and repeat with the remaining bread, adding 1/2 tablespoon of butter for each batch.
    

---

### Recipe Notes

- **Milk Temperature:** Warming the milk to roughly 80°F prevents the melted butter from clumping when it hits the cold liquid.
    
- **The "No-Egg" Flavor:** By using only yolks, you eliminate the sulfur compounds found in whites that often give French toast an "eggy" or "omelet" taste.
    
- **Bread Choice:** If you can't find Challah, use a firm, high-quality white bread like Arnold Country Classics or Pepperidge Farm Farmhouse.
