[[Recipes]]

**Serves:** 4–6

---

### Ingredients

#### For the Kefta:

- **1 lb** finely ground beef or lamb
    
- **1** onion, finely chopped or grated
    
- **A small bunch** of fresh flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
    
- **1–2 teaspoons** ground cinnamon
    
- **1 teaspoon** ground cumin
    
- **1 teaspoon** ground coriander
    
- **½ teaspoon** cayenne pepper, or **1 teaspoon** paprika
    
- **Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper**
    

#### For the Tagine:

- **1 tablespoon** olive oil
    
- **1 tablespoon** butter or ghee
    
- **1** onion, roughly chopped
    
- **2–3** garlic cloves, halved and crushed
    
- **A thumb-size piece** of fresh ginger, peeled and finely chopped
    
- **1** red chile, thinly sliced
    
- **2 teaspoons** ground turmeric
    
- **A small bunch** of cilantro, roughly chopped
    
- **A small bunch** of fresh mint leaves, chopped
    
- **Freshly squeezed juice** of 1 lemon
    
- **1** lemon, cut into 4 or 6 segments, with pips removed
    

---

### Instructions

1. **Prepare the Kefta:** In a bowl, pound the ground meat with your knuckles. Lift and slap the meat back into the bowl, then add the onion, parsley, cinnamon, cumin, coriander, cayenne, salt, and black pepper. Knead well by hand for a few minutes. Shape the mixture into approximately 16 walnut-sized balls. (These can be made 2–3 days ahead and refrigerated).
    
2. **Sauté Aromatics:** Heat the oil and butter together in a tagine or heavy-based casserole dish. Add the roughly chopped onion, garlic, ginger, and chile; sauté until they begin to brown.
    
3. **Simmer Base:** Add the turmeric, half of the cilantro, and half of the mint. Pour in roughly $1\frac{1}{4}$ cups of water and bring to a boil. Reduce the heat and simmer, covered, for 10 minutes.
    
4. **Poach Kefta:** Carefully place the meatball (kefta) into the liquid, cover, and poach for about 15 minutes. Roll them in the liquid occasionally to ensure they cook evenly on all sides.
    
5. **Final Simmer:** Pour over the lemon juice, season the liquid with salt, and tuck the lemon segments around the kefta. Poach for an additional 10 minutes.
    
6. **Serve:** Sprinkle with the remaining cilantro and mint, and serve hot.
