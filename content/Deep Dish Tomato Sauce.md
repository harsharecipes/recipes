## Homemade Deep Dish Pizza

**Yield:** Approx. 12 servings

**Prep time:** 30 minutes | **Rise time:** 1 hour 15 minutes | **Bake time:** 35 minutes

### Ingredients

**For the Dough**

- **4 cups** all-purpose flour
    
- **3 tbsp** yellow cornmeal
    
- **1 3/4 tsp** salt
    
- **2 3/4 tsp** instant yeast
    
- **1 cup + 2 tbsp** lukewarm water
    
- **5 tbsp** butter, melted
    
- **2 tbsp** olive oil
    
- **2 tbsp** vegetable oil
    

**For the Bulk Deep Dish Sauce (4x Batch)**

- **8 tbsp** (1 stick) unsalted butter
    
- **1 cup** grated onion
    
- **2 tsp** dried oregano
    
- **16 cloves** garlic, minced
    
- **4 cans (28 oz each)** crushed tomatoes
    
- **2 tsp** sugar
    
- **8 tbsp** fresh basil leaves, chopped
    
- **4 tbsp** extra-virgin olive oil
    
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
    

**For Assembly**

- **1 lb** shredded mozzarella cheese
    
- **1/4 cup** grated Parmesan cheese
    
- **Toppings of choice:** Pepperoni, cooked sausage, peppers, etc.
    

---

### Directions

#### 1. Prepare the Dough

Mix all dough ingredients in a bowl. Knead by hand or with a stand mixer (medium-low speed) for about **7 minutes**. Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover, and let rise in a warm spot for **60 minutes** until puffy.

#### 2. Simmer the Sauce

While the dough rises, melt the butter in a large saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the onion, oregano, and a pinch of salt. Sauté for **5 minutes** until translucent. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant. Stir in the crushed tomatoes and sugar. Bring to a simmer, then drop heat to medium-low. Simmer for **30–35 minutes** until the sauce reduces significantly (it should be thick enough to stay put on a pizza).

#### 3. Prep the Pans

Grease a 14-inch deep-dish pan, a large cast-iron skillet, or two 9-inch springform pans with cooking spray. Drizzle **3–4 tbsp** of oil into the bottom, coating the base and lower sides evenly.

#### 4. Shape and Par-Bake

- **Preheat oven to 425°F.**
    
- Stretch the dough into a large circle and lay it in the pan. If it shrinks back, cover and let it rest for **15 minutes**.
    
- Press the dough across the bottom and up the sides of the pan. Let it rest again for **10–15 minutes**.
    
- **Par-bake** the crust for **10 minutes** until it just starts to show color.
    

#### 5. Finish the Sauce

Remove the thickened sauce from the heat. Stir in the fresh basil and extra-virgin olive oil. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

#### 6. Assemble and Bake

Remove the par-baked crust from the oven. Layer the bottom with the **shredded mozzarella** and your chosen toppings. Spoon the sauce over the top, leaving a small amount of "lip" at the top of the crust. Sprinkle with **Parmesan cheese**.

#### 7. Final Bake

Bake for **25 minutes** until the crust is golden-brown and the cheese is bubbling. **Crucial Step:** Let the pizza cool for **15 minutes** before slicing to allow the structure to set.

### Pro-Tip

Since you're making a massive batch of sauce, you'll likely have leftovers unless you are making multiple pizzas. This sauce freezes beautifully in airtight containers for up to 3 months!
