## Essential Roasted Tomatillo-Serrano Salsa

**Yield:** Makes about $2\frac{1}{2}$ cups

### Ingredients

- **1 pound (10 to 12 medium)** tomatillos, husked and rinsed
    
- **Fresh serrano chiles to taste** (roughly 5, about 1 ounce total)
    
- **2 large** garlic cloves, unpeeled
    
- **1 small (4-ounce)** white onion, finely chopped
    
- **1/4 cup** loosely packed, roughly chopped cilantro
    
- **Salt**, about 1 generous teaspoon
    
- **Sugar**, about 1 scant teaspoon (if needed)
    

---

### Directions

#### 1. Roast the Key Ingredients

- **Tomatillos:** Lay the tomatillos on a baking sheet and place 4 inches below a very hot broiler. Roast until they blister, blacken, and soften on one side (about 5 minutes), then turn them over and roast the other side. Let them cool completely on the baking sheet.
    
- **Chiles and Garlic:** Roast on an ungreased griddle or heavy skillet over medium heat, turning occasionally, until blackened in spots and soft. This takes 5 to 10 minutes for the chiles and about 15 minutes for the garlic. Cool, then pull stems from chiles and peel the garlic.
    

#### 2. Create the Puree

- Scrape the roasted tomatillos and any accumulated juices into a food processor or blender along with the peeled garlic and chiles.
    
- Pulse the machine until the mixture is reduced to a coarse-textured puree.
    
- Scrape the salsa into a serving bowl.
    

#### 3. Season and Finish

- Stir in between $1/4$ and $1/2$ cup water to reach an easily spoonable consistency.
    
- Place the chopped onion in a strainer, rinse under cold water, shake off excess moisture, and stir into the salsa along with the chopped cilantro.
    
- Taste and season with salt and a little sugar.
    

---

### Additional Notes

- **Advance Preparation:** While the puree can be made a day or more ahead, the finished salsa should be eaten within several hours after adding the fresh onion and cilantro.
    
- **Substitutions:** Fresh jalapeños can be used to replace the serranos if preferred.
    
- **Quick Version (Salsa Verde Cruda):** For an all-raw version, coarsely puree $1/2$ pound of raw tomatillos with the rest of the ingredients (all raw) and 1–2 tablespoons of water. This version should be enjoyed within an hour.
