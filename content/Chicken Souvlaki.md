This Grilled Chicken Souvlaki recipe focuses on achieving charred, lemony chicken paired with a mild, creamy tzatziki.

## Grilled Chicken Souvlaki

**Yield:** Serves 4

### Ingredients

**Tzatziki Sauce**

- **1 tbsp** lemon juice
    
- **1** small garlic clove, minced to paste
    
- **3/4 cup** plain Greek yogurt
    
- **1/2** cucumber, peeled, halved lengthwise, seeded, and diced fine (1/2 cup)
    
- **3 tbsp** minced fresh mint
    
- **1 tbsp** minced fresh parsley
    
- **1/8 tsp** salt
    

**Chicken and Skewers**

- **Salt and pepper**
    
- **1 1/2 lbs** boneless, skinless chicken breasts, trimmed and cut into 1-inch pieces
    
- **1/3 cup** extra-virgin olive oil
    
- **2 tbsp** minced fresh parsley
    
- **1 tsp** finely grated lemon zest plus **1/4 cup** juice (2 lemons)
    
- **1 tsp** honey
    
- **1 tsp** dried oregano
    
- **1** green bell pepper, quartered, stemmed, seeded, and each quarter cut into 4 chunks
    
- **1** small red onion, ends trimmed, peeled, halved lengthwise, and each half cut into 4 chunks
    
- **4 (8-inch)** pita breads
    

---

### Directions

#### 1. Make the Tzatziki Sauce

Whisk the lemon juice and garlic together in a small bowl and let stand for **10 minutes**. Stir in the yogurt, cucumber, mint, parsley, and salt. Cover and set aside.

#### 2. Brine and Marinate the Chicken

Dissolve 2 tablespoons of salt in 1 quart of cold water. Submerge the chicken in the brine, cover, and refrigerate for **30 minutes**. Meanwhile, whisk together the olive oil, parsley, lemon zest and juice, honey, oregano, and 1/2 teaspoon pepper in a medium bowl. Transfer **1/4 cup** of this oil mixture to a large bowl and set aside for later.

#### 3. Assemble the Skewers and Prep Pitas

Remove chicken from brine and pat dry. Toss the chicken with the remaining oil mixture in the medium bowl. Thread the skewers by alternating 4 pieces of bell pepper (concave side up), 4 pieces of chicken, and 2 chunks of onion on each of four 12-inch metal skewers. Lightly moisten 2 pitas with water and sandwich them between 2 unmoistened pitas; wrap the stack tightly in greased heavy-duty foil.

#### 4. Prepare the Grill

- **Charcoal:** Light a large chimney of charcoal. When covered with ash, spread over half the grill. Heat the rack until hot (about 5 minutes).
    
- **Gas:** Turn all burners to high, cover, and heat until hot (about 15 minutes). Leave the primary burner on high and turn off the others.
    

#### 5. Grill the Skewers

Place skewers on the hotter side of the grill. Cook, turning occasionally, until well browned and the chicken registers **160°F** (about **15 to 20 minutes**). Use a fork to push the chicken and vegetables into the bowl with the reserved oil mixture. Stir gently, cover with foil, and let rest for **5 minutes**.

#### 6. Heat Pitas and Serve

Place the pita packet on the cooler side of the grill for about **5 minutes**, flipping occasionally. Spread each warm pita with 2 tablespoons of tzatziki. Place a quarter of the chicken and vegetables in the center, roll into a cylinder, and serve.
