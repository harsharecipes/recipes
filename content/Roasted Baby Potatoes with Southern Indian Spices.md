## Roasted Baby Potatoes with Southern Indian Spices

**Serves:** 8

---

### Ingredients

- **1/4 cup canola oil**, plus extra for greasing baking dish
    
- **3 pounds baby red potatoes**, rinsed and halved (larger potatoes quartered)
    
- **20 curry leaves** (optional)
    
- **6 dried red chiles**
    
- **2 teaspoons mustard seeds**
    
- **1 1/2 teaspoons cumin seeds**
    
- **A 1-inch piece fresh ginger**, peeled and finely minced
    
- **3 garlic cloves**, peeled and finely minced
    
- **1/2 jalapeño**, cored and seeded for milder flavor, finely chopped
    
- **1 teaspoon turmeric**
    
- **1 tablespoon kosher salt**
    
- **1/2 cup chopped fresh cilantro**
    
- **1/2 cup chopped fresh mint leaves**
    
- **1 lime**, half juiced and the other half cut into wedges for serving
    
- **1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper**
    

---

### Instructions

1. **Prep the Oven and Potatoes:** Preheat the oven to 400°F. Grease a large, deep baking dish with oil, add the potatoes, and set aside.
    
2. **Bloom the Spices:** Heat the oil with the curry leaves (if using), chiles, mustard seeds, and cumin seeds in a large skillet until the mustard seeds start to crackle and pop, about 1 1/2 minutes.
    
3. **Aromatics:** Stir in the ginger, garlic, and jalapeño. Cook, stirring often, until the garlic is fragrant (about 1 minute).
    
4. **Finish the Oil:** Add the turmeric and salt and cook for 1 minute. Pour the spice mixture over the potatoes and stir until evenly coated.
    
5. **Roast:** Bake the potatoes until tender and browned, 25 to 40 minutes, stirring every 10 minutes.
    
6. **Garnish:** Remove the baking dish from the oven and stir in the cilantro, mint, lime juice, and cayenne pepper.
    
7. **Serve:** Serve hot with lime wedges.
