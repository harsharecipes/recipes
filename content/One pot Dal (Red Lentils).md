## One-Pot Creamy Masoor Dal

This is the ultimate comfort food—a simple, warming red lentil dal finished with a golden, fragrant tadka.

---

## **Ingredients**

**The Dal Base**

- **1 cup** red lentils (masoor dal), rinsed
    
- **5 cups** water (see note on heat sources)
    
- **2** juicy tomatoes, chopped
    
- **1½ tsp** turmeric powder
    
- **1 tsp** ginger-garlic paste
    
- **Salt** to taste
    

**The Tadka (Tempering)**

- **1 knob** of butter (or ghee)
    
- **1 small** red onion, finely sliced
    
- **4–5** garlic cloves, sliced
    
- **1 tsp** cumin seeds
    
- **1 pinch** of hing (asafoetida), optional
    

---

## **Method**

1. **Boil:** Combine the lentils, water, tomatoes, turmeric, and ginger-garlic paste in a large pot. Bring to a rolling boil.
    
2. **Simmer:** Reduce the heat to low, cover the pot, and let it cook gently for **20 minutes**.
    
3. **Cream:** Once the lentils are soft, stir and lightly mash them with the back of a spoon to achieve a smooth, creamy texture. Season with salt.
    
4. **Temper:** In a small frying pan, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the onion, garlic, cumin, and hing. Fry until the onions are golden brown and fragrant.
    
5. **Infuse:** Pour the hot tadka directly over the dal. Immediately cover the pot with a lid for **one minute** to trap the aromatic oils and infuse the flavor.
    

---

## **Important: Heat Source Adjustments**

The water ratio and timing above are optimized for **gas stovetops**.

- **For Induction or Electric:** Heat is applied primarily from the base, meaning water evaporates more slowly.
    
- **Adjustment:** Start with **3.5–4 cups** of water instead. Extend the cooking time by **5–10 minutes** and check the consistency, adding more water only if necessary.
