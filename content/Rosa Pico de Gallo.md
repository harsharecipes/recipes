# Rosa Pico de Gallo

Pico de Gallo (Rosa Mexicano)


2 Ripe medium tomatoes (about 12 oz or 4-5 plum toms)

1/4 cup finely chopped white onion

12 sprigs cilantro with stems removed (finely chopped)

1 jalapeño or 1 serrano, diced

1 tsp salt

Juice of one lime

1 tsp of olive oil (optional)


Core tomatoes and cut them in half. Gently squeeze out most of the seeds, and cut them into a 1/4-inch dice. Toss the diced tomatoes, onions, cilantro and jalapeño together in a mixing bowl. Add the salt and lime juice, then stir in the oil (if using). Let sit for a few minutes. Can be kept up to 4 hours at room temp.
