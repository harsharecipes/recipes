[[Recipes]]


**Serves 4**

These quick-cooking boneless pork chops are seasoned with a tangy-sweet glaze made from orange juice, lime, and chipotle chile powder.

---

### **Ingredients**

- **Glaze:**
    
    - **⅔ cup** orange juice
        
    - **1 ½ tablespoons** coconut sugar
        
    - **½ teaspoon** grated lime zest plus **1 teaspoon** juice
        
    - **½ teaspoon** chipotle chile powder
        
- **Chops:**
    
    - **4 (8-ounce)** boneless pork chops, ¾ to 1 inch thick, trimmed
        
    - **Kosher salt and pepper**
        
    - **1 tablespoon** extra-virgin olive oil
        

---

### **Directions**

#### **1. Prepare the Glaze**

Combine the orange juice, coconut sugar, lime zest and juice, and chipotle chile powder in a bowl.

#### **2. Prepare and Sear the Chops**

To prevent the chops from curling, cut 2 slits about 2 inches apart through the outer layer of fat and silverskin on each chop. Pat the chops dry with paper towels and season them with salt and pepper. Heat the oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat until just smoking. Place the chops in the skillet and cook until well browned on the first side (about 5 minutes).

#### **3. Simmer in Glaze**

Flip the chops and add the glaze to the skillet. Reduce the heat to medium-low and cook until the pork registers 145°F (5 to 8 minutes).

#### **4. Reduce and Serve**

Transfer the chops to a serving platter and tent loosely with aluminum foil. Increase the heat to medium and simmer the glaze, adding any accumulated pork juices, until it is thick and syrupy (2 to 6 minutes). Pour the glaze over the chops and serve.

---

### **Variations**

- **Maple-Glazed Pork Chops:** Substitute the orange glaze with a mixture of ½ cup maple syrup, ¼ cup cider vinegar, 2 teaspoons Dijon mustard, and 2 teaspoons minced fresh thyme. Simmer as directed.
