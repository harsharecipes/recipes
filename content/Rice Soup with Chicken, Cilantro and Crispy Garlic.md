## Rice Soup with Chicken, Cilantro, and Crispy Garlic (_Kao Tome Gai_)

**Serves:** 4 to 6

---

### Ingredients

- **3 tablespoons vegetable oil**
    
- **2 tablespoons coarsely chopped garlic**
    
- **4 cups chicken stock**
    
- **2 cups cooked rice**
    
- **1/4 pound ground pork, chicken, or turkey**
    
- **1 tablespoon fish sauce**
    
- **1 teaspoon freshly ground pepper**
    
- **1 tablespoon minced fresh ginger (optional)**
    
- **3 green onions, thinly sliced crosswise**
    
- **A handful of fresh cilantro leaves, coarsely chopped**
    

---

### Instructions

1. **Prepare Crispy Garlic:** Place a small heatproof bowl near the stove. Heat the oil in a small frying pan over medium heat until a bit of garlic sizzles instantly. Add the remaining garlic and cook, stirring often, until fragrant and golden (about 1 to 2 minutes). Remove from heat, pour the hot garlic and oil into the heatproof bowl, and set aside to cool.
    
2. **Boil Stock and Rice:** In a medium saucepan, bring the chicken stock to a boil over medium-high heat. Stir in the cooked rice.
    
3. **Cook Meat:** Once the soup returns to a boil, add the ground meat in generous pinches by hand or teaspoon to form free-form meatballs. Stir well and add the fish sauce. Simmer for 3 to 4 minutes until the meat is fully cooked.
    
4. **Garnish and Serve:** Transfer the hot soup to a serving bowl. Top with the prepared crispy garlic in oil, ground pepper, ginger (if using), green onions, and cilantro. Stir well just before serving hot.
