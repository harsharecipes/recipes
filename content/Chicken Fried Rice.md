[[Recipes]]

**Yield:** Serves 2-4

**Active Time:** 15 minutes

**Total Time:** 20 minutes

---

### Ingredients

- **1/2 pound boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breast**, thinly sliced against the grain
- **3-4 cups cooked jasmine rice**, preferably day-old and refrigerated
- **2 large eggs**, lightly beaten
- **2 teaspoons minced garlic**
- **1 teaspoon minced ginger**
- **2-3 scallions**, whites and greens separated and thinly sliced
- **1/2 cup frozen peas and carrots** (optional)
- **1 tablespoon soy sauce**
- **1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil**
- **1/2 teaspoon sugar**
- **1/4 teaspoon ground white pepper**
- **3-4 tablespoons neutral oil** (such as canola or vegetable), divided
- **Kosher salt**, to taste

---

### Procedures

1. **Marinate the Chicken:** In a small bowl, toss the sliced chicken with a splash of soy sauce and a teaspoon of oil. Let it sit while you prepare the remaining ingredients.
    
2. **Sear the Chicken:** Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a wok or large non-stick skillet over high heat until smoking. Add the chicken in a single layer and let sear without moving for 1 minute. Stir-fry until just cooked through, about 2 minutes total. Transfer to a plate and set aside.
    
3. **Cook the Eggs:** Wipe out the pan if necessary and add another tablespoon of oil. Pour in the beaten eggs; they should puff immediately. Stir-fry until just set into large curds, then transfer to the plate with the chicken.
    
4. **Fry the Rice:** Add the remaining 2 tablespoons of oil to the pan and heat until smoking. Add the rice and spread it out, letting it sit for 30 seconds to develop a light char. Stir-fry, breaking up any clumps with a spatula, for 2–3 minutes until the grains are toasted and "jumping" in the pan.
    
5. **Sauté Aromatics:** Push the rice to the sides of the pan to create a well. Add the garlic, ginger, and scallion whites to the center. Cook until fragrant (about 30 seconds), then toss to incorporate with the rice.
    
6. **Final Toss:** Return the chicken, eggs, and frozen vegetables (if using) to the pan. Drizzle the soy sauce around the edges of the pan to caramelize it slightly before tossing everything together.
    
7. **Season and Finish:** Add the scallion greens, sesame oil, sugar, and white pepper. Toss over high heat for 30 seconds to 1 minute until everything is well-combined and piping hot. Taste and add salt if needed. Serve immediately.
