[[Recipes]]

**Serves:** 8

---

### Ingredients

#### For the Goat Cheese Pesto

- 1½ cups pine nuts
    
- 2 loosely packed cups baby spinach
    
- 1 loosely packed cup basil leaves
    
- 4 ounces creamy chèvre-style goat cheese
    
- ½ cup golden raisins
    
- 1 tablespoon plus 1½ teaspoons extra-virgin olive oil
    
- ¾ teaspoon Garam Masala
    
- ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper
    
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
    
- ¾ teaspoon ground peppercorns
    

#### For the Marinade

- 3 tablespoons chickpea flour (besan)
    
- ½ cup sour cream
    
- 2 garlic cloves, peeled and finely chopped
    
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
    
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt
    
- ½ teaspoon ground peppercorns
    
- ½ teaspoon turmeric
    
- ¼ teaspoon Garam Masala
    

#### The Chicken

- 4 large boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 7 to 8 ounces each), halved crosswise
    

---

### Instructions

1. **Preheat and Toast:** Preheat the oven to 400°F. Place the pine nuts on a rimmed baking sheet and toast until browned, 4 to 6 minutes, shaking the baking sheet midway through. Set aside to cool.
    
2. **Make the Pesto:** Transfer the cooled nuts to a food processor. Add the spinach, basil, goat cheese, raisins, oil, Garam Masala, cayenne pepper, salt, and ground peppercorns. Pulse until fairly smooth and set aside.
    
3. **Prepare the Marinade:** Heat the chickpea flour in a medium skillet over medium-high heat until it is browned, stirring often (2 to 3 minutes). If it gets too dark, lift the skillet to cool while stirring. Transfer the toasted flour to a medium bowl and whisk in the remaining marinade ingredients.
    
4. **Prep the Chicken:** Lay a long sheet of plastic wrap on your work surface. Place a chicken breast half in the center and fold the plastic over it. Using the flat side of a meat mallet or the bottom of a heavy skillet, pound the chicken until it is 1/2 to 1/4 inch thick.
    
5. **Stuff and Fold:** Remove the top sheet of plastic and add 1 to 3 tablespoons of pesto to the middle of the chicken. Use the plastic wrap to fold the chicken flaps over the filling into a packet. Place it seam-side down in a baking dish. Repeat with the remaining chicken.
    
6. **Marinate:** Spread the marinade over the chicken, ensuring it gets all over and in between each rolled breast. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes or overnight.
    
7. **Bake:** Bake until the chicken is completely cooked through, 25 to 30 minutes, and serve.
