[[Recipes]]

Roasting cauliflower is one of the easiest ways to prepare this heady vegetable, and the addition of brown butter and Pecorino enhances its nutty and hearty profile. Red onions provide a necessary acidity to lighten the dish, while mint brightens every bite.

**Serves:** 4 as a small plate

---

### Ingredients

- **4 tablespoons unsalted butter**
    
- **1 small head cauliflower**, cut into florets (approximately 4–5 cups)
    
- **1/2 teaspoon kosher salt**
    
- **1/2 cup thinly shaved red onion**
    
- **1/4 cup grated Pecorino**
    
- **10 mint leaves**, rolled and thinly sliced (chiffonade)
    
- **1 tablespoon lemon juice**
    
- **Kosher salt**, to taste
    

---

### Steps

1. **Preheat:** Preheat the oven to **500°F**.
    
2. **Brown the Butter:** In a stainless steel sauté pan (essential for seeing the color change), melt the butter over medium heat. Whisk constantly as little brown bits begin to form. Continue whisking until the butter turns light brown and smells nutty, then remove from heat.
    
3. **Toss and Roast:** In a large glass mixing bowl, toss the cauliflower florets with the brown butter and 1/2 teaspoon salt. Transfer to a baking sheet and roast for **15 minutes** or until just tender.
    
4. **Assemble:** In a large mixing bowl, toss the hot cauliflower with the red onion, Pecorino, and mint.
    
5. **Season:** Drizzle lemon juice over the cauliflower and season with additional kosher salt to taste.
    
6. **Serve:** Transfer to a dish and serve hot.
