[[Recipes]]

**Servings:** 6–8

---

### Ingredients

- **2 teaspoons smoked paprika**
    
- **1 teaspoon cayenne pepper**
    
- **6 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil**
    
- **2 (15-oz.) cans chickpeas**, rinsed, drained, and patted very dry
    
- **Kosher salt**, to taste
    
- **2 teaspoons finely grated lime zest**
    

---

### Preparation

1. **Mix Spices:** Combine the smoked paprika and cayenne pepper in a small bowl and set aside.
    
2. **Heat Oil:** Heat the olive oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat.
    
3. **Fry:** Working in two batches, add the chickpeas to the skillet and sauté, stirring frequently, until golden and crispy (about 15–20 minutes).
    
4. **Drain:** Using a slotted spoon, transfer the chickpeas to paper towels to drain briefly.
    
5. **Season:** Place the chickpeas in a medium bowl. Sprinkle the paprika and cayenne mixture over them and toss to coat. Season to taste with salt.
    
6. **Finish:** Toss with the lime zest and serve.
