**Posted by Carrie Vasios Mullins**

**Adapted from Mark Bittman**

Brown butter gives this cornbread a warm, nutty flavor.

- **Yield:** Makes 8 servings
    
- **Active Time:** 15 minutes
    
- **Total Time:** 40 minutes
    

---

### Ingredients

- **4 tablespoons unsalted butter**
    
- **1 1/4 cups whole milk**
    
- **2 large eggs**
    
- **1 1/2 cups coarse cornmeal**
    
- **1/2 cup all-purpose flour**
    
- **1/2 cup sugar**
    
- **1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder**
    
- **1 teaspoon salt**
    

---

### Procedures

1. **Preheat:** Preheat oven to 375°F.
    
2. **Brown the Butter:** In a small skillet, melt the butter over medium heat. Continue to cook after melting, stirring frequently, until the butter has browned (about 3 minutes). Transfer the brown butter to a cake pan.
    
3. **Mix Wet Ingredients:** In a small bowl, whisk together the eggs and milk.
    
4. **Mix Dry Ingredients:** In a large bowl, whisk together the cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt. Stir in the egg mixture until combined.
    
5. **Bake:** Pour the batter into the prepared pan and smooth out the top. Bake until golden and a cake tester comes out clean, about 25 minutes.
    

---

> **Note:** If you have a cast-iron skillet, you can use it to make the cornbread. Simply melt and brown the butter directly in the skillet and proceed with the recipe as directed.
