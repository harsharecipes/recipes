# Cheesy Baked Pasta With Sausage and Ricotta Recipe (with Video) • 5★ • 45 min

## 📖 Description
This is a hearty Italian-American pasta dish featuring sausage and tomatoes, cooked together in a Dutch oven. Its uniqueness lies in the one-pot cooking method where the pasta cooks directly in the sauce, absorbing all the rich flavors.

## 🛒 Ingredients
- 1.5 pounds sausage
- 1 tsp fennel
- red pepper flakes (to taste)
- 28 oz can tomatoes
- 16 oz rotini pasta
- 1.5 cups water
- basil (optional, for garnish)

## 🍳 Instructions
1. Brown the sausage in a Dutch oven. Drain excess fat.
2. Add fennel, red pepper flakes, and tomatoes to the Dutch oven.
3. Stir in the rotini pasta and water.
4. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 23 minutes, stirring occasionally.
5. Remove from heat and let rest, covered, for 5 minutes.
6. Garnish with fresh basil if desired.
7. Serve with roasted vegetables or salad, and bread.
