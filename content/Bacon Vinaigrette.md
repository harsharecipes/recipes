[[Recipes]]

**Recipe courtesy of Alton Brown**

**Total Time:** 20 min

**Prep:** 10 min

**Cook:** 10 min

**Yield:** 8 servings

**Level:** Easy

---

### Ingredients

- **1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil**
    
- **1/4 cup cider vinegar**
    
- **2 tablespoons bacon drippings**
    
- **1 tablespoon dark brown sugar**
    
- **1 tablespoon prepared mustard**
    
- **1 teaspoon salt**
    
- **1 teaspoon freshly ground pepper**
    
- **2 heads radicchio, quartered**
    

---

### Directions

1. **Make the Vinaigrette:** In a small non-reactive bowl, combine all ingredients except for the radicchio. Whisk until an emulsion is formed and reserve at room temperature.
    
2. **Grill the Radicchio:** Place the quartered radicchio onto a hot grill or grill pan. Cook on each side for approximately 2 minutes per side until the edges are slightly wilted.
    
3. **Steam:** Remove the radicchio from the grill and place it onto a plate. Cover with a stainless steel bowl and allow the steam to continue cooking the radicchio for 5 minutes.
    
4. **Serve:** Drizzle the vinaigrette over the radicchio wedges and serve immediately.
