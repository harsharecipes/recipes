## Easy One-Pot Chicken Tinga

**By J. Kenji López-Alt**

This streamlined version of a Mexican classic delivers juicy, smoky, and spicy shredded chicken. By poaching bone-in chicken to a precise temperature and finishing it in a concentrated purée, you achieve maximum flavor with minimal effort.

---

### **Recipe Stats**

- **Yield:** Makes about 4 cups (serves 4)
    
- **Active Time:** 35 minutes
    
- **Total Time:** 1 hour 10 minutes
    
- **Special Equipment:** Instant-read thermometer, immersion blender (or countertop blender)
    

---

### **Ingredients**

- **2** bone-in, skin-on chicken breast halves (about 1 ¼ pounds)
    
- **Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper**
    
- **2 tbsp** lard or vegetable oil
    
- **6 oz** tomatillos, peeled (about 2 medium)
    
- **6 oz** ripe plum tomatoes (about 2 medium)
    
- **4 cloves** garlic
    
- **1 small** white onion, finely chopped
    
- **2 tsp** dried oregano (preferably Mexican)
    
- **2** bay leaves
    
- **2 tbsp** cider vinegar
    
- **2 cups** chicken stock or water
    
- **2 to 3** canned chipotle chilies in adobo sauce, plus **1 tbsp** sauce
    
- **2 tsp** Asian fish sauce
    

---

### **Instructions**

#### **1. Sear the Chicken and Aromatics**

Season the chicken generously with salt and pepper. Heat oil in a medium saucepan over high heat until shimmering. Cook chicken skin side down until well browned (6–8 minutes), then flip and cook for 2 minutes longer. Transfer chicken to a plate. Immediately add the tomatillos, tomatoes, and garlic to the pot and cook until blistered and browned in spots (about 5 minutes).

#### **2. Simmer and Poach**

Add the onion and cook until softened (2 minutes). Stir in the oregano and bay leaves for 30 seconds. Add the vinegar and stock, then return the chicken to the pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a bare simmer. Cover and cook until the chicken reaches **145°F** on an instant-read thermometer (20–30 minutes).

#### **3. Reduce and Purée**

Transfer the chicken to a bowl to cool. Continue cooking the sauce at a hard simmer until reduced by half (about 5 minutes). Remove the bay leaves and add the chipotle chilies and adobo sauce. Blend the sauce using an immersion blender until relatively smooth.

#### **4. Shred and Combine**

When the chicken is cool enough to handle, discard the skin and bones and finely shred the meat. Return the shredded chicken to the sauce and stir in the fish sauce. Bring to a simmer and cook until the sauce thickens and coats the chicken. The result should be very moist but not soupy. Season with additional salt and pepper if needed.

---

### **Serving Suggestions**

Serve your tinga in tacos, on top of crispy tostadas or nachos, or stuffed into hearty burritos and enchiladas.
