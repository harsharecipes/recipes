## Latin-Style Chicken and Cauliflower Rice

**Serves 4**

This Latin-inspired one-pan meal pairs browned chicken breasts with tender cauliflower rice, finished with briny olives and fresh tomatoes.

---

### **Ingredients**

- **1 head cauliflower (2 pounds)**, cored and cut into 1-inch florets (approx. 6 cups)
    
- **4 (6- to 8-ounce) boneless, skinless chicken breasts**, trimmed
    
- **Kosher salt and pepper**
    
- **3 tablespoons** extra-virgin olive oil
    
- **1 onion**, chopped fine
    
- **1 tablespoon** tomato paste
    
- **1 tablespoon** minced fresh oregano (or 1 teaspoon dried)
    
- **2 garlic cloves**, minced
    
- **2 teaspoons** smoked paprika
    
- **⅛ teaspoon** cayenne pepper
    
- **½ cup** Paleo Chicken Broth
    
- **1 tomato**, cored and chopped
    
- **½ cup** pitted large green olives, chopped coarse
    
- **3 tablespoons** minced fresh cilantro
    

---

### **Instructions**

#### **1. Prepare the Cauliflower**

Working in two batches, pulse cauliflower in a food processor 4 to 6 times until finely ground into 1/4- to 1/8-inch pieces, scraping down the bowl as needed. Transfer to a bowl.

#### **2. Prep and Sear Chicken**

Cover chicken with plastic wrap and pound to an even thickness. Pat dry with paper towels and season with salt and pepper. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat until smoking. Brown chicken on one side (about 4 minutes), then transfer to a plate.

#### **3. Sauté and Simmer**

Add remaining 2 tablespoons oil to the skillet over medium heat. Add onion and 1 teaspoon salt; cook until softened (about 5 minutes). Stir in tomato paste, oregano, garlic, paprika, and cayenne; cook until fragrant (about 1 minute). Stir in broth, scraping up browned bits, then stir in the cauliflower. Nestle chicken browned side up into the skillet with any juices. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and cook until chicken reaches 160°F (10 to 12 minutes).

#### **4. Finish and Serve**

Transfer chicken to a cutting board to rest. Stir tomatoes and olives into the cauliflower rice and cook until the rice is tender and the mixture is nearly dry (about 5 minutes). Season with salt and pepper to taste. Transfer cauliflower rice to a platter, slice chicken into 1/2-inch pieces to arrange on top, and sprinkle with cilantro.
