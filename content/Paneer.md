## Homemade Paneer

**A simple, fresh Indian cheese**

Making your own paneer is surprisingly easy and far superior to store-bought versions. This mild, firm cheese doesn't melt, making it perfect for soaking up the vibrant flavors of curries and gravies.

---

### **Recipe Stats**

- **Yield:** Approximately 10 oz of cheese
    
- **Prep Time:** 5 minutes
    
- **Cook/Press Time:** 1 hour 15 minutes
    
- **Dietary:** Vegetarian, Gluten-Free
    

---

### **Ingredients & Equipment**

- **½ gallon** whole milk
    
- **2–3 tbsp** lemon juice (plus more if needed)
    
- **Salt**, to taste
    
- **Equipment:** Large pot, whisk, colander, cheesecloth
    

---

### **Instructions**

#### **1. Heat the Milk**

Pour the milk into a large pot. Heat over medium-high, stirring frequently with a whisk and scraping the bottom to prevent scorching. Bring the milk just to a boil.

> **Watch closely:** Remove from heat immediately once it starts to bubble to prevent a boil-over mess!

#### **2. Curdle the Milk**

Once off the heat, add the lemon juice and stir gently. The milk will begin to separate into solid white **curds** and thin, yellowish **whey**. Continue stirring until separation is complete. If the liquid remains milky, add a little more lemon juice until it clears.

#### **3. Strain and Season**

Line a colander with two layers of cheesecloth and place it in the sink (or over a bowl if you wish to save the whey). Pour the mixture into the cloth. While the curds are draining, stir in a pinch of salt.

#### **4. Squeeze and Shape**

Once the curds are cool enough to handle, gather the corners of the cheesecloth and squeeze out as much excess liquid as possible. Flatten the bundle into a disk approximately **1-inch thick** and place it in a flat bowl or on a rimmed plate.

#### **5. Press the Paneer**

Place another flat bowl or plate on top of the cheesecloth bundle. Weight it down with a heavy object, such as a large can of tomatoes or a heavy jar. Let it press for at least **1 hour** to firm up.

#### **6. Serve or Store**

Your paneer is now ready to be cubed and added to your favorite dishes!

---

### **Storage & Usage**

- **Store:** Keep refrigerated in an airtight container for up to one week.
    
- **Dish Ideas:** Perfect for _Mattar Paneer_ (peas), _Saag Paneer_ (spinach), or _Paneer Butter Masala_.
