## Shredded Beef with Tomatoes and Chiles

**Servings:** 10

---

### Ingredients

- **1 1/4 lbs boneless beef chuck steaks**, cut into 4 pieces
    
- **3 small white onions**, diced
    
- **4 garlic cloves**, peeled and finely chopped
    
- **1 tablespoon vegetable oil**
    
- **1 (28 ounce) can whole tomatoes with juice**, drained and chopped
    
- **2–3 serranos**, stemmed, seeded, and very finely chopped
    
- **Salt**, to taste (about 1/2 teaspoon for seasoning)
    

---

### Directions

1. **Simmer the Beef:** In a medium saucepan over medium heat, combine the beef with 2 quarts of salted water, approximately 1/3 of the diced onions, and half of the garlic. Simmer until the meat is very tender, about 1 1/2 hours.
    
2. **Shred the Meat:** Strain the mixture, reserving the broth for another use. Once the meat is cool enough to handle, shred it into coarse strands using your fingers or two forks (bits of onion and garlic mixed in are fine).
    
3. **Sauté Aromatics:** Wash and dry the saucepan, then return it to medium heat with 1 tablespoon of oil. When the oil is hot, add half of the remaining onions and cook until golden, about 6 minutes. Stir in the remaining garlic and cook for 1 minute more.
    
4. **Cook Tomatoes and Chiles:** Add the chopped tomatoes and serrano chiles to the pan. Cook until most of the liquid has evaporated, about 3 minutes.
    
5. **Finish:** Stir the shredded meat into the tomato mixture and simmer for a few minutes. Taste and season with approximately 1/2 teaspoon of salt. Remove from the heat and serve.
