[[Recipes]]

This recipe combines traditional flavors with a hybrid cooking method, roasting the meatballs for a better crust before finishing them in a savory vegetable broth.

---

### **Ingredients**

#### **Meatballs**

- **12 oz** ground beef
    
- **1/3 cup** grated Parmesan cheese, plus more for serving
    
- **1/3 cup** fresh bread crumbs
    
- **1/4 cup** water
    
- **2 tbsp** chopped fresh parsley
    
- **1 clove** garlic, minced
    
- **1 3/4 tsp** kosher salt, plus more for seasoning
    
- **1/2 tsp** dried oregano
    
- **1/4 tsp** freshly ground black pepper, plus a few grinds
    

#### **Soup Base**

- **2 1/2 tbsp** olive oil
    
- **1** onion, chopped
    
- **2** carrots, peeled and chopped
    
- **2 ribs** celery, chopped
    
- **8 cups** chicken broth
    
- **3 sprigs** thyme
    
- **10 oz** baby spinach
    
- **1 cup** small pasta
    
- **1 tbsp** lemon juice
    
- **Garnish:** Chopped parsley and extra grated Parmesan cheese
    

---

### **Directions**

#### **1. Prep the Meatballs**

Preheat your oven to **375°F**. In a mixing bowl, combine the ground beef, bread crumbs, 1/3 cup Parmesan, water, garlic, oregano, parsley, 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt, and 1/4 teaspoon pepper. Form the mixture into 1-inch meatballs (roughly 25–35 total) and place them on a baking sheet. Refrigerate until ready to bake.

#### **2. Sauté the Aromatics**

Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add the onion, carrots, and celery. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables have softened and are just beginning to brown (about 10 minutes).

#### **3. Simmer the Broth**

Add the chicken broth and thyme sprigs to the pot and bring the liquid to a simmer.

#### **4. Roast and Combine**

Place the meatballs in the preheated oven and bake for **12 minutes**. Once finished, carefully transfer the meatballs into the simmering soup and cook for an additional **5 minutes**.

#### **5. Final Additions**

Stir in the small pasta and baby spinach. Add the lemon juice and season with additional salt and pepper to taste.

#### **6. Serve**

Ladle into bowls and serve garnished with fresh parsley and a generous sprinkle of Parmesan cheese.
