## Easy Thai Peanut Sauce (Thai Satay Sauce)

This is a traditional Thai-style satay sauce that is sweet and fragrant, similar to Thai curry but without the heat. It does not contain sesame oil, hoisin, or chili sauce.

- **Prep time:** 8 mins
    
- **Cook time:** 5 mins
    
- **Total time:** 13 mins
    
- **Yield:** 3.5 cups
    

---

### Ingredients

- **13.5-ounce can full-fat coconut milk**
    
- **2 ounces (approx. ¼ cup) Thai red or Massaman curry paste**
    
- **¾ cup unsweetened (natural) creamy peanut butter** (must be the type with oil on top and no added sugar/emulsifiers)
    
- **½ tablespoon salt**
    
- **¾ cup sugar**
    
- **2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar or white vinegar**
    
- **½ cup water**
    

---

### Instructions

1. **Combine:** Place all ingredients into a medium, heavy-bottomed pot.
    
2. **Boil:** Bring the mixture to a very gentle boil over medium heat while whisking constantly.
    
3. **Simmer:** Reduce heat to low and simmer for 3–5 minutes. Stir frequently to ensure the mixture does not scorch at the bottom.
    
4. **Cool and Serve:** Remove from heat and let the sauce cool to room temperature or slightly warmer. Serve with satay or fried tofu.
    

---

### Notes

- **Storage:** The sauce keeps for weeks in the refrigerator in a glass container or can be frozen.
    
- **Reheating:** Refrigerated sauce will thicken; thin it with a little water and reheat before serving.
    
- **Curry Paste:** Maesri red curry paste is recommended, though Mae Ploy (hotter) or Massaman paste can also be used.
    
- **Peanut Options:** If natural peanut butter is unavailable, you can grind 12 ounces of roasted peanuts into a paste using a mortar and pestle or food processor.
