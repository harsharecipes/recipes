[[Recipes]]

Yield:** 4 servings

---

### Ingredients

- **2 cucumbers**, peeled and thinly sliced
    
- **1/2 red onion**, thinly sliced
    
- **1 1/2 cups water**
    
- **1/2 cup rice wine vinegar**
    
- **1 teaspoon white sugar**
    
- **1/4 teaspoon seasoning salt**
    

---

### Instructions

1. **Prep Vegetables:** Place the thinly sliced cucumbers and red onions in a medium bowl or shallow dish.
    
2. **Mix Dressing:** In a separate container, whisk together the water, rice wine vinegar, sugar, and seasoning salt until the sugar is dissolved.
    
3. **Combine:** Pour the liquid mixture over the cucumbers and onions.
    
4. **Marinate:** For the best flavor, let the salad sit in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes to allow the vegetables to pick up the tang of the vinegar.
