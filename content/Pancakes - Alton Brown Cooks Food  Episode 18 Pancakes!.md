# Alton Brown Cooks Food | Episode 18: Pancakes!

## 📖 Description
This recipe is for homemade pancakes using a dry mix that can be prepared in advance. It uniquely emphasizes precise weight measurements for dry ingredients for consistent results.

## 🛒 Ingredients
- 30 g sugar
- 12 g baking powder
- 9 g baking soda
- 9 g salt
- 840 g all-purpose flour
- 50 g neutral oil
- 2 large eggs
- 480 g buttermilk

## 🍳 Instructions
1. Combine dry ingredients for the mix: sugar, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and flour. Weigh each ingredient accurately.
2. Store the dry mix in an airtight container.
3. When ready to make pancakes, combine wet ingredients: oil, eggs, and buttermilk. Whisk until emulsified.
4. Place a mixing bowl on a scale and tear to zero. Sift in 280g of the dry pancake mix.
5. Add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients.
6. Mix gently with a spatula or wooden spoon until just combined (no more than 12 stirs). Do not overmix.
7. Heat a griddle to 350°F (175°C).
8. Lightly grease the griddle.
9. Pour 1/4 cup (2 oz) of batter per pancake onto the hot griddle.
10. Cook for 3-4 minutes, or until bubbles form around the edges and the surface begins to set.
11. Flip the pancakes and cook for another 2 minutes, or until golden brown and cooked through.
12. Repeat with remaining batter.
13. Keep pancakes warm in a low oven on a rack with a damp towel if desired.
