# Carolina Pulled Pork

Mike’s Carolina Pulled Pork


Ingredients:

Dry Rub:

- ¼ Cup Black Pepper

- ¼ Cup Brown Sugar

- ¼ Cup Paprika

- 2 tsp Dry Mustard

- 1 tsp Celery Salt

- 1 tsp Cayenne Pepper

- ½ tsp Garlic Salt

- ½ tsp Onion Salt

- ¼ tsp Salt

- 1 Whole Pork Butt


Carolina Sauce:

- 2 Cups Cider Vinegar

- 2 Cups Chicken Stock

- 1 Cup Dry White Wine

- ½ Cup Ketchup

- ½ Cup Brown Sugar

- ¼ Cup Molasses

- 4 tsp Crushed Red Pepper

- 3 tsp Salt

- 1 tsp Black Pepper

- 1 tsp White Pepper

- 2 cloves Chopped Garlic


Directions:


1. Mix all dry rub ingredients in a bowl. Generously rub Pork, place in plastic bag and refrigerate overnight.


2. Take out pork and rub again.


3. Let sit at room temp. for 2 hours. (step not necessary if pressed for time)


4. Roast uncovered in oven at 250F degrees until tender, about 8-9 hours. Internal Temperature should be ~195F degrees


5. For Sauce - bring all ingredients to a boil, reduce to simmer for at least 30 min.


6. Allow pork to sit for at least 20 min. after it’s removed from oven (this step is optional but it allows pork to tenderize more) Pull Pork into pieces and toss with Sauce. Serve on rolls. Enjoy!
