[[Recipes]]

**By Sasha Marx**

- **Yield:** Serves 4
    
- **Active Time:** 30 minutes
    
- **Total Time:** 45 minutes
    

### Ingredients

**For the Taleggio Sauce**

- **8 oz (225g)** Taleggio cheese, rind removed, cut into 1/2-inch pieces (chill before cutting)
    
- **1/2 cup (120ml)** heavy cream
    

**For the Broccoli & Gremolata**

- **2 heads** broccoli (about 2 lbs)
    
- **1/3 cup (80ml)** extra-virgin olive oil
    
- **1/3 cup (15g)** fresh flat-leaf parsley, chopped
    
- **1 small** green jalapeño, seeded and finely minced
    
- **1 medium** clove garlic, minced
    
- **1 lemon**, finely grated zest only
    
- **3 tbsp (45ml)** vegetable oil (plus more as needed)
    
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
    

---

### Directions

#### 1. Prepare the Cheese Sauce

Place the Taleggio pieces in a heat-proof bowl or tall liquid measuring cup. Bring the heavy cream to a simmer in a small saucepan over medium-high heat. Pour the hot cream over the cheese and immediately cover the bowl tightly with plastic wrap. Let it sit for **20 minutes**. Use an immersion blender to process until perfectly smooth (the consistency should mimic nacho cheese). Set aside.

#### 2. Prep the Broccoli "Steaks"

Separate the broccoli crowns from the stems. Slice the crowns lengthwise into **3/4-inch-thick steaks**, ensuring each piece has two flat sides for searing. Set aside.

- **The "No-Waste" Tip:** Take any small floret scraps and finely chop them. Peel the tough outer layer off the remaining stems and finely dice them. Place these trimmings into a small mixing bowl.
    

#### 3. Mix the Gremolata

To the bowl with the chopped stems and floret scraps, add the olive oil, parsley, minced jalapeño, garlic, and lemon zest. Stir well and season with salt and pepper to taste.

#### 4. Char the Broccoli

Heat 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil in a large cast iron skillet over high heat until smoking. Add the broccoli steaks (work in batches if needed). Use a weight or a smaller skillet to press the broccoli down for even contact. Cook until deeply charred (**3–4 minutes**). Flip, add the remaining oil, and cook the other side until charred and the stems are fork-tender (**3–4 minutes**). Transfer to paper towels and season with salt.

#### 5. Assembly

Spread a generous layer of the warm Taleggio sauce onto a serving platter. Arrange the charred broccoli steaks on top and spoon the broccoli-stem gremolata over everything. Serve immediately.

---

### Tips & Storage

- **Pro Tip:** Taleggio is much easier to slice if you pop it in the freezer for 10 minutes beforehand.
    
- **Reheating Sauce:** If the sauce thickens, microwave it in 15-second bursts, stirring in between. Avoid overheating, or the cheese may break (separate).
    
- **Make-Ahead:** The sauce can be stored in the fridge for up to **5 days**.
