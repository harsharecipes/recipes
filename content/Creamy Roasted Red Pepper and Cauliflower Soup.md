[[Recipes]]

**By Deborah Mele**

This vibrant soup features roasted red peppers and tender cauliflower, finished with a bright touch of lemon and a savory pancetta-apple garnish.

- **Yield:** Serves 4 to 6
    
- **Active Time:** 30 minutes
    
- **Total Time:** 1 hour
    

---

### Ingredients

**For the Soup:**

- **4 large sweet red bell peppers**, cored, stemmed, seeded, and pressed flat
    
- **2 tablespoons olive oil**
    
- **1 small onion**, chopped (about 3/4 cup)
    
- **2 medium garlic cloves**, chopped (about 2 teaspoons)
    
- **1 teaspoon Kosher salt**, plus more to taste
    
- **1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper**, plus more to taste
    
- **1/2 teaspoon hot red pepper flakes**
    
- **1 teaspoon finely chopped fresh thyme leaves**
    
- **1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika** (optional)
    
- **1 quart homemade or store-bought low-sodium chicken stock** (or vegetable broth for vegetarian)
    
- **1 medium russet potato**, peeled and chopped (about 1 cup)
    
- **1 head of cauliflower**, core removed, cut into florets
    
- **1 teaspoon freshly squeezed juice from 1 lemon**
    

**For the Garnish:**

- **2 tablespoons olive oil**
    
- **1/3 cup pancetta**, finely chopped (omit for vegetarian)
    
- **1/2 medium apple**, peeled, cored, and cut into 1/2 inch dice
    
- **2 slices of crusty Italian bread**, cut into 1/2 inch dice
    
- **Black pepper**, to taste
    

---

### Procedures

1. **Roast the Peppers:** Preheat the broiler to high. Place peppers on a foil-lined baking sheet about 4 inches below the heating element. Broil until blistered and blackened. Place in a plastic bag to rest for 15 minutes, then peel off the skin under running water once cool. Coarsely chop and set aside.
    
2. **Sauté Aromatics:** Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a heavy saucepan over medium-low heat. Cook the onion until tender (about 4 minutes), then add garlic and cook for 1 minute more until fragrant.
    
3. **Simmer:** Stir in the salt, black pepper, red pepper flakes, thyme, paprika, and broth. Bring to a boil, then lower to a simmer. Add the potato and cauliflower; cook until tender, about 20 minutes.
    
4. **Purée:** Let the soup cool for 15 minutes. Working in batches, purée the soup and the roasted peppers in a blender or food processor until very smooth.
    
5. **Finish Soup:** Stir in the lemon juice and season to taste with additional salt and pepper. Keep warm on low heat.
    
6. **Prepare Garnish:** Heat the remaining 2 tablespoons of oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add pancetta and apples; cook until apples are tender and pancetta begins to brown (3 to 4 minutes). Add bread dice and cook until crisp and browned (about 4 minutes longer). Season with black pepper.
    
7. **Serve:** Ladle soup into bowls and top with a scoop of the pancetta-apple croutons. Serve immediately.
    

---

> **Tip:** Don't skip the lemon juice! It brings a vibrancy that helps the flavors of the cauliflower and peppers truly pop.
