[[Recipes]]
This Peruvian-style grilled chicken (often called _Pollo a la Brasa_) is famous for its crispy skin, smoky cumin-rubbed meat, and that addictive, creamy green sauce (_Aji Verde_).

---

## Peruvian-Style Grilled Chicken with Green Sauce

- **Prep time:** 30 mins
    
- **Cook time:** 45 mins
    
- **Resting time:** 5 mins
    
- **Total time:** 80 mins
    
- **Serves:** 4
    

---

### Ingredients

#### For the Green Sauce

- **3 whole** jalapeño chiles, roughly chopped (see notes)
    
- **1 tbsp** ají amarillo pepper paste (optional)
    
- **1 cup** fresh cilantro leaves (1 oz / 28g)
    
- **2 medium** cloves garlic
    
- **1/2 cup** mayonnaise
    
- **1/4 cup** sour cream
    
- **2 tsp** fresh lime juice
    
- **1 tsp** distilled white vinegar
    
- **2 tbsp** extra-virgin olive oil
    
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
    

#### For the Chicken

- **1 whole chicken** (3 1/2 to 4 lbs)
    
- **4 tsp** kosher salt
    
- **2 tbsp** ground cumin
    
- **2 tbsp** paprika
    
- **1 tsp** freshly ground black pepper
    
- **3 medium** cloves garlic, minced
    
- **2 tbsp** white vinegar
    
- **2 tbsp** vegetable or canola oil
    

---

### Special Equipment

- Grill (charcoal or gas)
    
- Blender
    
- Instant-read thermometer
    
- Metal or wooden skewer
    

---

### Directions

#### 1. Prepare the Green Sauce

1. **Blend:** Combine jalapeños, ají amarillo, cilantro, garlic, mayonnaise, sour cream, lime juice, and vinegar in a blender.
    
2. **Emulsify:** Blend on high speed until smooth. With the motor running, slowly drizzle in the olive oil.
    
3. **Season & Chill:** Season with salt and pepper. Transfer to a sealed container and refrigerate; the sauce will thicken as it sits.
    

#### 2. Prep the Chicken (Spatchcocking)

1. **Remove Backbone:** Pat chicken dry. Using kitchen shears, cut along both sides of the backbone to remove it.
    
2. **Flatten:** Turn the chicken breast-side up and press down firmly on the breastbone until it cracks and lays flat.
    
3. **Secure:** For stability, run a skewer horizontally through the chicken, entering one thigh, passing through both breasts, and exiting the other thigh. Tuck wing tips behind the back.
    
4. **Rub:** Mix salt, cumin, paprika, pepper, garlic, vinegar, and oil into a paste. Rub it evenly over all surfaces of the chicken.
    

#### 3. Grill the Bird

1. **Setup:** Prepare a grill for **two-zone cooking** (coals on one side, or half the gas burners on high). Clean and oil the grates.
    
2. **Indirect Heat:** Place chicken **skin-side up** on the cooler side of the grill, with legs facing the heat. Cover and cook until the breast reaches **110°F (43°C)**.
    
3. **Sear:** Flip the chicken **skin-side down** over the hot side. Press down with a spatula for good contact. Cover and cook until the skin is crisp and the breast reaches **145–150°F (63–66°C)**.
    
    > **Note:** If it starts to char too quickly, move it back to the cool side to finish.
    

#### 4. Rest and Serve

1. Transfer chicken to a board and let it rest for **5 to 10 minutes**.
    
2. Carve into pieces and serve warm with plenty of green sauce.
    

---

### Chef's Notes

- **Heat Control:** For a milder sauce, remove the seeds and ribs from the jalapeños before blending.
    
- **Ají Amarillo:** This is a Peruvian yellow pepper paste found in Latin markets. It adds authentic fruitiness, but the recipe still works great if omitted.
