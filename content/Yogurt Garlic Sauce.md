[[Recipes]]

**Makes about $1\frac{1}{4}$ cups**

---

### Ingredients

- **$\frac{1}{2}$ large hothouse cucumber**, peeled
    
- **3 garlic cloves**, minced
    
- **$\frac{3}{4}$ cup Greek yogurt**
    
- **1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice**
    
- **Coarse salt and freshly ground pepper**
    

---

### Instructions

1. **Prepare for Draining:** Line a colander with a double layer of cheesecloth and place it over a deep bowl to catch liquid.
    
2. **Process and Drain:** Roughly chop the cucumber and place it in a food processor with the minced garlic. Process until finely chopped, then transfer the mixture to the lined colander to drain for approximately 30 minutes.
    
3. **Extract Juice:** Gather the ends of the cheesecloth and twist tightly to force any remaining juices into the bowl. Discard the cucumber solids and cheesecloth, keeping the collected juice in the bowl.
    
4. **Combine and Season:** Place the yogurt in a nonreactive mixing bowl. Whisk in the reserved cucumber-garlic juice and the lemon juice until it reaches the consistency of a creamy vinaigrette (you may not need all the juice). Season to taste with salt and pepper.
    
5. **Store or Serve:** Serve immediately or store in a covered nonreactive container in the refrigerator for up to 1 day.
