[[Recipes]]

This fresh, creamy dressing pairs perfectly with an Escarole Salad.

**Yield:** 1 serving

---

### Ingredients

- **2 tablespoons buttermilk**
    
- **1 tablespoon plus 1 teaspoon lemon juice**
    
- **Zest from 1/4 lemon**
    
- **1 1/2 teaspoons chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley**
    
- **1 1/2 teaspoons chopped fresh chives**
    
- **1/2 teaspoon chopped fresh tarragon**
    
- **2 tablespoons olive oil**
    
- **Coarse salt and freshly ground pepper**, to taste
    

---

### Directions

1. **Whisk Base:** In a small bowl, whisk together the buttermilk, lemon juice, lemon zest, parsley, chives, and tarragon.
    
2. **Emulsify:** Slowly whisk in the olive oil until well combined.
    
3. **Season:** Season with salt and pepper to taste.
