[[Recipes]]

These buttery, tender rolls combine the technique of pull-apart garlic knots with classic Thanksgiving stuffing flavors.

- **Yield:** Serves 8 to 10
    
- **Active Time:** 30 minutes
    
- **Total Time:** 5 hours (or up to overnight)
    

---

### Special Equipment

- 9- by 13-inch casserole dish
    
- Large skillet
    

---

### Ingredients

- **4 tablespoons butter**, divided
    
- **8 ounces sage sausage or breakfast sausage**, removed from casings (or substitute with finely diced bacon, pancetta, or chorizo)
    
- **1 small onion**, finely chopped (about 3/4 cup)
    
- **1 stalk celery**, finely chopped (about 1/2 cup)
    
- **4 medium cloves garlic**, finely chopped
    
- **1/4 cup minced fresh sage leaves**
    
- **1/4 cup minced fresh parsley leaves**
    
- **Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper**
    
- **1 pound homemade or store-bought pizza dough**
    
- **2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil**
    

---

### Procedures

1. **Sauté the Filling:** Melt 2 tablespoons of butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the sausage and break it into fine pieces (no larger than 1/4-inch) using a whisk or potato masher. Cook until only a few bits of pink remain (about 8 minutes). Add the onion, celery, garlic, and sage; cook until the vegetables are softened (about 10 minutes). Stir in the parsley and season with salt and pepper. Transfer to a large bowl and let cool completely.
    
2. **Form the Knots:** On a lightly floured surface, divide the dough into two even pieces. Stretch each piece into an 8-by-4-inch strip and cut crosswise into 12 smaller strips (24 strips total). Tie each strip into a simple knot.
    
3. **Coat and Rise:** Add the dough knots to the bowl with the cooled sausage mixture. Toss by hand until every knot is thoroughly coated. Grease the casserole dish with 1 tablespoon of butter and arrange the knots in a single layer. Drizzle with olive oil, cover tightly with plastic wrap, and let rise until doubled in size (about 4 hours at room temperature or 12 to 16 hours in the refrigerator).
    
4. **Bake:** Preheat the oven to 425°F with the rack in the center position. Unwrap the rolls and bake until golden brown and crisp, approximately 25 to 30 minutes.
    
5. **Finish:** Just before removing the rolls from the oven, melt the remaining tablespoon of butter. Brush the hot rolls immediately with the melted butter. Serve warm with gravy and cranberry sauce.
